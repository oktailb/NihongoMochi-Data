"Due to" (Formal): ～につき

The expression ～につき is a very formal way to state a reason or cause. It is almost exclusively used in written notices, announcements, or business signs. It translates to "due to..." or "on account of..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach につき directly to a Noun.

[Noun] + につき

  • 工事中こうじちゅうにつき、立たち入いり禁止きんし。
    (Due to construction, entry is forbidden.)
  • 雨天うてんにつき、試合しあいは中止ちゅうしします。
    (On account of rain, the match is cancelled.)
  • ただいま清掃中せいそうちゅうにつき、ご利用りよういただけません。
    (Currently due to cleaning, it cannot be used.)

2. Other Meaning: "Per" (Amount)

In a different context, につき can also mean "per" or "each."

  • 一人ひとりにつき千円せんえんです。
    (It is 1,000 yen per person.)

3. Distinction from ～ので

While ～ので is used in daily speech and polite writing, ～につき is much more concise and rigid. You will rarely hear it in casual conversation; it is meant for public information.
