Logical Conclusion: ～わけです / ～わけだ

The expression ～わけです is used to state a logical conclusion or a natural consequence based on a certain situation. It translates to "that means...", "no wonder...", or "it is only natural that..."

1. Reaching a Conclusion

Use this when you realize why something is the way it is after hearing an explanation or seeing a fact.

  • 「かれは日本にほんに十年じゅうねん住すんでいたんですよ。」
    「だから日本語にほんごが上手なわけですね。」
    ("He lived in Japan for ten years." "No wonder his Japanese is so good [that explains it].")
  • 五パーセント引びきということは、九十五円きゅうじゅうごえんというわけだ。
    (A 5% discount means it is 95 yen.)

2. Natural Consequence

It can also express that something is an obvious result of circumstances.

  • 暑あついわけだ。三十五度さんじゅうどもある。
    (No wonder it's hot. It's 35 degrees!)

3. Connection Table

Type Connection
Verbs / い-Adj Plain Form + わけだ
な-Adj Adj + な + わけだ
Nouns Noun + (という) + わけだ

4. Distinction from ～んです

  • ～んです: Provides a reason or explanation for a visible state.
  • ～わけです: Emphasizes the logic or process of thinking that led to the conclusion.
