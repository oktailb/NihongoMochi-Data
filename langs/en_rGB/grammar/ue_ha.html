Since the Decision is Made: ～上は (うえは)

When you have committed to a certain path or when a situation has reached a point of no return, there's only one thing to do: move forward! ～上は means "since," "now that," or "so long as." It's used when a fact creates a logical obligation or determination.

1. Sentence Structure

Verb (Dictionary/Past Form) + 上は

2. Resolute Examples

  • 約束やくそくした上は、必ず守ります。
    (Now that I've made a promise, I will definitely keep it.)
  • やるからには、勝かつ上は徹底的にやりたい。
    (Since I'm doing it [since the situation is such], I want to do it thoroughly to win.)
  • 社長しゃちょうがそう言いう上は、従うしかない。
    (Now that the president has said so, we have no choice but to obey.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a "high-determination" word. It's similar to kara ni wa. It says: "Because Factor X exists as a solid reality, I am logically bound to Factor Y." Use it when you want to show that your mind is made up and you're ready for the consequences!
