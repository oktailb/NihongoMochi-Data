In Response To: ～に応えて (にこたえて)

When an action is taken specifically to satisfy a request, a hope, or a demand, we use ～に応えて. It means "in response to" or "to meet the expectations of." It shows a direct reaction to an external stimulus.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + に応えて

2. Responsive Examples

  • 客きゃくの要望ようぼうに応えて、新あたらしい商品しょうひんを作つくった。
    (In response to the customers' requests, we created a new product.)
  • アンコールに応えて、歌手かしゅはもう一曲歌うたった。
    (Responding to the encore, the singer sang one more song.)
  • 国民こくみんの期待きたいに応えて、選手せんしゅたちは頑張がんばった。
    (Meeting the expectations of the nation, the athletes did their best.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a "proactive" response. It implies that there was some pressure or expectation from others, and the subject acted to fulfill it. It's often used in news, marketing, and formal descriptions of events.
