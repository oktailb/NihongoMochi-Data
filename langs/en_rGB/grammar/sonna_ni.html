Not That Much: そんなに

When you want to say something isn't quite as intense or extreme as suggested, we use そんなに. When paired with a negative verb or adjective, it means "not that much," "not so," or "not particularly."

1. Sentence Structure

そんなに + [Verb/Adj Negative Form]

2. Moderate Examples

  • このテストは、そんなに難むずかしくない。
    (This test isn't that difficult.)
  • 今日は、そんなに忙いそがしくありません。
    (I'm not that busy today.)
  • そんなに心配しんぱいしなくてもいいですよ。
    (You don't have to worry that much.)

3. Sensei's Note

そんなに literally means "to that extent." It's often used when responding to someone who might be exaggerating or worrying too much. It brings the conversation back to a more balanced, moderate level. It's the "don't overthink it" of Japanese modifiers!
