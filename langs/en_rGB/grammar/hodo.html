Extent and Comparison: ～ほど

The word ほど (often written as 程) is a versatile tool for measuring the "depth" of a situation. It describes the extent or degree of something, often by comparing it to something else.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Noun + ほど
  • Verb (Plain Form) + ほど
  • A は B ほど [Negative Adj] (A is not as... as B)

2. Descriptive Examples

  • 涙なみだが出でるほどうれしかった。
    (I was so happy [to the extent] that tears came out.)
  • 今年ことしの冬ふゆは去年きょねんほど寒さむくない。
    (This winter is not as cold as last year.)
  • 彼かれほどの才能さいのうがある人ひとはいない。
    (There is no one with as much talent as him.)

3. Sensei's Note

When used with a negative adjective, ほど creates a comparison of "not as much as." In its other role, it emphasizes the intensity of an action: "It was so X that Y!" Think of it as painting a picture of just how intense a feeling or situation is.
