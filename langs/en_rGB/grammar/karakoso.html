The Ultimate Reason: ～からこそ

When you want to say "Precisely because of X," and not for any other reason, we use ～からこそ. The こそ particle adds a powerful emphasis, highlighting that the reason given is the exact one that matters.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (Plain Form) + からこそ
  • Noun + だからこそ
  • い-Adj + からこそ
  • な-Adj + だからこそ / であるからこそ

2. Powerful Examples

  • 大変たいへんだからこそ、やりがいがあるのです。
    (It is precisely because it's difficult that it's worth doing!)
  • あなたのことを思おもっているからこそ、注意ちゅういしたんです。
    (I warned you precisely because I'm thinking of you/care about you.)
  • 努力どりょくしたからこそ、合格ごうかくできた。
    (I was able to pass precisely because I put in the effort.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is much stronger than a simple kara. It's often used when the result might seem contradictory to the reason (like finding joy in hardship). It says: "Don't look at anything else; this is the reason why!"
