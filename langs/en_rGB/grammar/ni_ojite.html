Adapting to Change: ～に応じて (におうじて)

The world is constantly shifting, and we must change with it! ～に応じて (written as ～に応じて) means "in accordance with," "depending on," or "to suit." it describes an action that adjusts itself to match a specific situation or variable.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + に応じて / に応じた

2. Adaptive Examples

  • 体力たいりょくに応じて、運動うんどうのメニューを決きめます。
    (I will decide the exercise menu according to [my] physical strength.)
  • 天候てんこうに応じて、服ふくを選えらびましょう。
    (Let's choose clothes depending on the weather.)
  • 客きゃくの人数にんずうに応じて、部屋へやを準備じゅんびする。
    (To prepare the rooms in accordance with the number of guests.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is all about flexibility! If you do the same thing every time, you don't need this word. But if your actions change when Factor X changes, then ～に応じて is your best friend. It shows a logical, reactive adaptation!
