Endless Silence: ～きり

This expression is used when something happens once and then... nothing. No change, no repeat. It's like a door that closed and never reopened. It's often used with the past tense to say "Since that time, it's been only that way."

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (Ta-form) + きり
  • Noun + きり

2. Lingering Examples

  • 彼かれには、１０年ねん前まえに会あったきりです。
    (I haven't seen him since we met 10 years ago [that was the last time].)
  • 息子むすこは部屋へやに入はいったきり、出でてこない。
    (My son went into his room and hasn't come out since.)
  • 二人きりで話はなしたい。
    (I want to talk with just the two of us.)

3. Sensei's Note

When used with nouns (like futari-kiri), it means "only" or "just." When used with verbs, it emphasizes the continuation of a state after a single event. It often carries a slightly sad or lonely feeling, like something was left unfinished or unchanged for a long time.
