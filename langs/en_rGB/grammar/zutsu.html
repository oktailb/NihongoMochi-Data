Distribution: ～ずつ

The suffix ～ずつ is used to indicate that a certain amount is distributed equally or that an action is performed in equal increments. It translates to "each," "at a time," or "by [amount]."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach ずつ directly after a counter or a quantity.

[Number + Counter] + ずつ

  • 一人ひとりにりんごを二ふたつずつあげました。
    (I gave two apples to each person.)
  • この薬くすりを一回いっかいに三錠さんじょうずつ飲のんでください。
    (Please take three tablets of this medicine at a time.)
  • 毎日まいにち少しすこしずつ漢字かんじを覚おぼえます。
    (I memorize Kanji little by little every day.)

2. Key Usage: Equal Portions

It ensures that the distribution is fair or the progress is steady. It is very common when giving instructions or describing habits.

3. Common Expressions

  • 一ひとつずつ (One by one)
  • 一歩いっぽずつ (Step by step)
  • 少すこしずつ (Little by little)
