Making Requests: ～てくれませんか

The expression ～てくれませんか is used to ask someone to do something for you. It is a polite request, suitable for friends, colleagues, or people you know slightly.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach くれませんか to the Te-form of a verb.

  • 塩しおを取とってくれませんか。
    (Could you please pass the salt?)
  • この漢字かんじの読よみ方かたを教おしえてくれませんか。
    (Could you please teach me how to read this Kanji?)

2. Politeness Levels

  • ～てくれる？ (Casual): To close friends or family.
  • ～てくれませんか (Polite): Standard polite request.
  • ～ていただけませんか (Very Polite): To superiors or strangers.

3. Nuance

This structure literally asks "Won't you give me the favor of...?", emphasizing that you are receiving a kind action from the other person.
