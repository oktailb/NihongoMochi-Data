Just as Expected: ～だけあって

When someone's performance or a thing's quality perfectly matches its reputation or status, we use ～だけあって. It means "Precisely because of X, it is naturally Y." It's a way of giving a compliment while acknowledging the reason behind the excellence.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Noun + だけあって
  • Verb/Adjective (Plain form) + だけあって

2. Respectful Examples

  • この肉にくは高たかいだけあって、とてもおいしい。
    (Being as expensive as it is, this meat is naturally delicious!)
  • 彼かれは一流いちりゅうの選手せんしゅだけあって、さすがに足あしが速はやい。
    (Just as you'd expect from a top-tier athlete, he is incredibly fast.)
  • １０年ねん日本にほんに住すんでいるだけあって、日本語にほんごがペラペラだ。
    (Precisely because he's lived in Japan for 10 years, his Japanese is fluent.)

3. Sensei's Note

This expression is almost always used for positive results. It's like saying "It lives up to its name!" If you want to say something is bad despite a good reason, you would use a different pattern like wari ni.
