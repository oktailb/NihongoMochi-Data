Absolute Denial: ～ものか / ～もんか

When someone suggests something that is completely out of the question, or when you are determined never to do something again, we use ～ものか. It is a very strong, often emotional, denial. It's like saying "As if I would!" or "Absolutely not!"

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb/Adj (Plain Form) + ものか / もんか
  • な-Adj + な + ものか / もんか
  • Noun + な + ものか / もんか

2. Resolute Examples

  • あんな店みせ、二度と行いくもんか！
    (As if I'd ever go to that shop again! / No way I'm going back!)
  • 彼かれが正直しょうじきなものか。いつも嘘うそばかりついている。
    (Honest? Him? No way! He's always telling lies.)
  • 負まけるもんか！最後さいごまで頑張がんばるぞ！
    (I won't lose! I'll fight until the end!)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very masculine and forceful way to speak in its "monka" form, but both genders use it to express strong feelings. It turns a question into a powerful "No!" Use it when you want to show your fighting spirit or complete rejection of an idea!
