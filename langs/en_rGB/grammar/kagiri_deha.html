"From what I...": ～かぎりでは

The expression ～かぎりでは (written as ～限りでは) is used to indicate that your statement is based on a limited scope of information, such as what you have seen, heard, or researched. It translates to "as far as...", "from what I...", or "within the scope of..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach かぎりでは to verbs related to perception or information gathering, such as 見るみる, 聞くきく, 調べるしらべる, or to nouns like 調査ちょうさ.

[Verb Dictionary / Past Form] + かぎりでは

  • 新聞しんぶんで読よんだかぎりでは、そのニュースは本当らしい。
    (As far as I read in the newspaper, that news seems to be true.)
  • 今回の調査ちょうさのかぎりでは、問題もんだいは見つかりませんでした。
    (Within the scope of this investigation, no problems were found.)
  • 私が知しっているかぎりでは、かれは独身です。
    (As far as I know, he is single.)

2. Key Nuance: Limiting Responsibility

Using ～かぎりでは shows that you are not claiming 100% certainty about the whole truth, but only reporting what is true within the information you currently possess.

3. Distinction from ～かぎり

  • ～かぎり: Focuses on a condition (as long as A is true, B happens).
  • ～かぎりでは: Focuses on the source of information (within the limits of what I know).
