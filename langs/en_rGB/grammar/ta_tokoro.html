Upon doing... (Result): ～たところ

The expression ～たところ is used when an action leads to an immediate or surprising result or discovery. It translates to "when I did...", "upon doing...", or "just as I finished..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach ところ directly to the Past Form (Ta-form) of a verb.

[Verb た形たけい] + ところ

  • 先生せんせいに相談そうだんしたところ、いいアドバイスをくれた。
    (When I consulted the teacher, [I found that] he gave me good advice.)
  • 新あたらしい薬くすりを飲のんだところ、すぐに良よくなりました。
    (Upon taking the new medicine, [it turned out that] I got better immediately.)
  • 駅えきに問とい合あわせたところ、忘れ物は届いていないと言われた。
    (When I inquired at the station, I was told that the lost item had not arrived.)

2. Key Nuance: Discovering a Result

This structure emphasizes the result that was revealed after performing an action. It's often used when you are reporting what happened or what someone else said after you did something.

3. Distinction from ～た時 (toki)

  • ～た時: Simply states the time when an action happened.
  • ～たところ: Focuses on the outcome or the new information learned from the action.
