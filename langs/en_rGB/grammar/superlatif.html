The Number One: 最も (もっとも) / 一番 (いちばん)

In Japanese, to express the "superlative" (the most...), we use words like 最も or 一番. While 一番 is the king of daily life, 最も is the champion of formal writing and speeches.

1. Sentence Structure

最も / 一番 + Adjective + Noun / Verb

2. Peak Examples

  • 富士山ふじさんは日本で最も高たかい山やまだ。
    (Mt. Fuji is the tallest mountain in Japan.)
  • これが世界せかいで一番有名ゆうめいな歌うたです。
    (This is the most famous song in the world.)
  • 彼かれが最も得意とくいな科目かもくは数学すうがくだ。
    (The subject he is most skilled at is mathematics.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of 一番 as the friendly, conversational "number one." Use 最も when you are writing an essay, giving a report, or want to sound more academic. Both of them clearly identify the "peak" of a category!
