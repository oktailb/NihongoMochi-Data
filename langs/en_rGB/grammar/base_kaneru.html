Polite Impossibility: ～かねる

The suffix ～かねる is a formal way to express that you are unable to do something, usually due to professional reasons, social etiquette, or psychological difficulty. It is much softer and more polite than saying dekimasen.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach かねる to the Verb Stem (the part before ~masu).

[Verb Stem] + かねる

  • その質問しつもんには、お答こたえかねます。
    (I am unable to [I must refrain from] answer that question.)
  • 個人情報こじんじょうほうについては、お教おしえかねます。
    (I cannot tell you regarding personal information.)
  • かれの意見いけんには、賛成さんせいしかねる。
    (I find it difficult to [cannot] agree with his opinion.)

2. Key Nuance: Professional Refusal

Using ～かねる implies that the speaker wants to help or agree, but due to certain circumstances or rules, it is impossible. It is the standard way to refuse a customer's request in a Japanese business setting.

3. Connection Example

言いう 言いい 言いいかねます
