Substitution: ～に代わって and ～かわりに

Both expressions deal with the concept of substitution or replacement, but they are used in slightly different contexts.

1. In place of (Someone): ～に代わって

This is used when someone performs a role or action on behalf of someone else. It is common in formal situations.

  • 入院中にゅういんちゅうの部長ぶちょうに代わって、私がごあいさつします。
    (In place of the manager who is hospitalized, I will give the greeting.)
  • 父ちちに代わって、お礼れいを申もうし上あげます。
    (On behalf of my father, I express my gratitude.)

2. Instead of / In exchange for: ～かわりに

This can mean "instead of doing X" or "in exchange for doing X." It is more versatile than ni kawatte.

  • 料理りょうりを作つくるかわりに、皿さらを洗あらってください。
    (In exchange for me cooking, please wash the dishes.)
  • コーヒーのかわりに、お茶ちゃを飲のみます。
    (I will drink tea instead of coffee.)

4. Summary of Difference

  • ～に代わって: "On behalf of" (Person A does what Person B usually does).
  • ～かわりに: "Instead of" (Option A is replaced by Option B) or "In compensation for."
