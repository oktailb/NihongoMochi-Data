Far from it: ～どころか

The expression ～どころか is used to emphasize that the actual situation is very different from what was just mentioned, often to a surprising or extreme degree. It translates to "far from...", "not just...", or "on the contrary."

1. Emphasizing a Greater Degree

It can be used to say that not only the first thing is true, but something even more extreme is true.

  • 独身どくしんどころか、子供こどもが三人さんにんもいます。
    (Far from being single, he has three children!)
  • 喉のどが痛いたいどころか、熱ねつもあって動うごけません。
    (Far from just having a sore throat, I have a fever and can't move.)

2. Emphasizing the Opposite

It can show that instead of result A, the opposite result B happened.

  • 貯金ちょきんどころか、借金しゃっきんがたくさんあります。
    (Far from having savings, I have a lot of debt.)

3. Connection

Attach どころか directly to nouns, adjectives, or the plain form of verbs.

Type Connection
Nouns / Adj / Verbs Plain Form + どころか

4. Quick Tip

Think of it as a way to say "Forget about A, the reality is B!". It always highlights a gap between expectation and reality.
