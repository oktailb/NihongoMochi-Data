The Inner Conflict: ～まいか

Sometimes your heart is pulled in two directions. ～まいか is used to express hesitation or a rhetorical question to oneself. It often appears in the form ～ようか～まいか, meaning "Whether I should or shouldn't..."

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb Volitional] + か + [Verb Dictionary + まい] + か

2. Hesitant Examples

  • 本当ほんとうのことを話はなそうかまいか、ずっと悩なやんでいた。
    (I was agonizing over whether to tell the truth or not.)
  • 買かおうかまいか、迷まよっている。
    (I'm wondering whether I should buy it or not.)
  • このまま待まっていようかまいか。
    (Should I stay and wait like this, or not...?)

3. Sensei's Note

This pattern perfectly captures the "tug-of-war" inside your mind. It's much more descriptive than just saying mayou (to be lost/hesitate). It shows the two specific options you are weighing against each other!
