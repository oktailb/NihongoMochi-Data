Contrary to: ～に反して

The expression ～に反して (にはんして) is used when a result or action goes against an expectation, a prediction, or a rule. It translates to "contrary to...", "against...", or "in opposition to..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach に反して directly to a Noun. To modify a following noun, use に反する.

[Noun] + に反して

  • 予想よそうに反して、テストは難むずかしくなかった。
    (Contrary to expectations, the test was not difficult.)
  • 親おやの期待きたいに反して、かれは大学だいがくに行いかなかった。
    (Against his parents' expectations, he did not go to university.)
  • 規則きそくに反する行為こういは許ゆるされません。
    (Actions that go against the rules are not permitted.)

2. Key Usage: Unmet Expectations

It is most commonly used with nouns representing predictions or hopes, such as 予想よそう (prediction), 期待きたい (expectation), or 意図いと (intention).
