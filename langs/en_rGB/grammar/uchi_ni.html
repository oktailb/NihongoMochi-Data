While / Before: ～うちに

The expression ～うちに has two main nuances: doing something before a situation changes, or a change happening naturally while an action is ongoing.

1. "Before the situation changes"

This is used when you want to perform an action while a certain condition still exists. It implies that if you wait too long, it will be too late.

  • 温あたたかいうちに、食たべてください。
    (Please eat it while it's still warm [before it gets cold].)
  • 独身どくしんのうちに、たくさん旅行りょこうしたいです。
    (I want to travel a lot while I'm still single.)
  • 忘わすれないうちに、メモをします。
    (I will take notes while I haven't forgotten [before I forget].)

2. "While something is happening (Natural change)"

This is used when a change occurs naturally or unconsciously during an ongoing state or action.

  • 音楽おんがくを聞きいているうちに、眠ねむってしまいました。
    (While I was listening to music, I [accidentally] fell asleep.)
  • 練習れんしゅうしているうちに、上手になりました。
    (While I was practicing, I became good at it.)
