Reporting Information: ～ということだ

The expression ～ということだ is used for two main purposes: to report information heard from another source ("I heard that...") or to explain the meaning of something ("It means that...").

1. Reporting News or Rumors

When used to report what you've heard, it is more formal than ～そうだ. It implies that the information comes from a reliable source or is a definitive statement.

  • ニュースによると、明日あしたは大雨おおあめということだ。
    (According to the news, [I heard that] it will be heavy rain tomorrow.)
  • 来月らいげつから物価ぶっかが上あがるということだ。
    (I heard that prices will rise from next month.)

2. Explaining Meaning

It can be used to summarize or rephrase what someone said, or to define a conclusion.

  • 「お金かねはいりません。」
    「つまり、無料むりょうということだね。」
    ("I don't need money." "In other words, it means it's free, right?")

3. Sentence Structure

Attach ということだ to the Plain Form of a sentence.

  • Verbs/Adjectives: Plain form + ということだ
  • Nouns: Noun + だ + ということだ

4. Variations

In casual speech, ということだ often becomes ってことだ.
