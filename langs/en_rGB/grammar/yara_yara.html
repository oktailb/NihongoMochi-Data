A Jumble of Examples: ～やら～やら

Sometimes life is so busy or messy that listing things feels like a jumble! ～やら～やら is used to list examples of things or actions, often with the nuance that there are many other things too, and the situation is a bit overwhelming or disorganized.

1. Sentence Structure

[Noun / Verb Plain] + やら + [Noun / Verb Plain] + やら

2. Chaotic Examples

  • 今日は宿題しゅくだいやら掃除そうじやらでとても忙いそがしい。
    (Today I'm so busy with things like homework, cleaning, and whatnot.)
  • 合格ごうかくして、うれしいやらびっくりするやらで大変だ。
    (Having passed, I'm a mix of happy, surprised, and all sorts of emotions!)
  • 机つくえの上うえには、本ほんやら消けしゴムやらが散らばっている。
    (On the desk, things like books and erasers are scattered all over.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of this as a "disorganized list." Unlike ~ya ~nado which feels orderly, ～やら～やら suggests a sense of chaos or that the speaker is overwhelmed by the variety. It's perfect for describing a busy day, a messy room, or a complex mix of feelings!
