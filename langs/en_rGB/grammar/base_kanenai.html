Risk of: ～かねない

The suffix ～かねない is used to express that there is a possibility or risk of a negative result happening. It translates to "could happen," "might possibly...", or "there's a risk that..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach かねない to the Verb Stem (the part before ~masu).

[Verb Stem] + かねない

  • そんなにスピードを出だすと、事故じこを起おこしかねない。
    (If you speed like that, you could possibly cause an accident.)
  • あまり無理むりをすると、病気びょうきになりかねませんよ。
    (If you overwork yourself too much, you risk getting sick.)
  • うっかりしていると、秘密ひみつを漏もらしかねない。
    (If you're careless, you might possibly leak the secret.)

2. Key Nuance: Negative Prediction

Unlike ～かもしれない (which is neutral), ～かねない is almost exclusively used for undesirable situations. It implies that if the current behavior continues, a disaster or mistake is likely.
