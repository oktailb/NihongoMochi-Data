Relative Standards: ～としては

How we judge something often depends on the category it belongs to. ～としては is used to state a standard or a role. It means "for a [Category]," or "as a [Role]." It's often used when a result is unexpected given the category.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + としては

2. Comparative Examples

  • 冬ふゆとしては、今日はとても暖あたたかい。
    (For [the standard of] winter, today is very warm.)
  • 初心者しょしんしゃとしては、かなり上手です。
    (For a beginner, they are quite skilled.)
  • 私わたしとしては、賛成できません。
    (As for me [from my standpoint/role], I cannot agree.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a great way to add perspective! It says "If you look at this thing as part of group X, then Y is true." It's different from a general toshite because the wa adds a sense of "within this specific scope." Use it to clarify your standards of judgment!
