Regardless of Factors: ～を問わず (をとわず)

Some rules are absolute and apply to everyone, everywhere! ～を問わず (written as ～を問わず) means "regardless of" or "without questioning." It is a formal way to say that a particular factor has no influence on a situation or requirement.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + を問わず

2. Universal Examples

  • この公園こうえんは昼夜ちゅうやを問わず、だれでも入はいれます。
    (This park is open to anyone, regardless of whether it's day or night.)
  • 経験けいけんの有無うむを問わず、募集ぼしゅうしています。
    (We are recruiting regardless of whether you have experience or not.)
  • 性別せいべつを問わず、平等びょうどうにチャンスがあります。
    (There are equal opportunities regardless of gender.)

3. Sensei's Note

The verb tou means "to ask" or "to question." So wo towazu literally means "not asking about X." It's very common in job advertisements, official rules, and public announcements. It creates a sense of fairness and broad inclusivity!
