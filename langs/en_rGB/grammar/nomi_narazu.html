Not only... but also: ～のみならず

The expression ～のみならず is a formal way to say "not only [A], but also [B]." It is commonly used in writing, news, and formal speeches.

1. Sentence Structure

It can be used with nouns, verbs, and adjectives. For nouns, it attaches directly (no da).

[A] のみならず、 [B] も ...

  • この映画えいがは子供こどものみならず、大人おとなも楽たのしめます。
    (This movie can be enjoyed not only by children but also by adults.)
  • 電気代でんきだいのみならず、ガス代だいも上あがりました。
    (Not only the electricity bill but also the gas bill has risen.)

2. Difference from ～だけでなく

  • ～だけでなく: The standard, everyday way to say "not only."
  • ～のみならず: Much more formal. It emphasizes that the scope is wider than expected.

3. Connection with Verbs and Adjectives

Connect it to the Plain Form.

  • かれは歌うたが上手じょうずなのみならず、ダンスもできます。
    (Not only is he good at singing, but he can also dance.)

4. Quick Tip

When using のみならず, the second part of the sentence almost always uses the particle も (also) to reinforce the addition.
