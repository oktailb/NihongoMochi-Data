Through and Throughout: ～を通じて and ～を通して

The expressions ～を通じて (をつうじて) and ～を通して (をとおして) are used to indicate a medium through which something happens, or a continuous period of time.

1. Through a Medium (Intermediary)

Use this when something is achieved via a person, organization, or method. They are often interchangeable in this context.

  • 友人ゆうじんを通じて、かれと知しり合あいました。
    (I got to know him through a friend.)
  • インターネットを通して、世界中せかいじゅうのニュースがわかる。
    (One can know the news of the whole world through the internet.)

2. Throughout a Period

Use this to show that a state exists or an action happens during an entire length of time.

  • この町まちは、一年いちねんを通じて観光客かんこうきゃくが訪おとずれる。
    (Tourists visit this town throughout the entire year.)
  • 四季しきを通して、いろいろな花はなが楽たのしめます。
    (You can enjoy various flowers throughout the four seasons.)

3. Nuance Difference

  • ～を通して: Often implies a more active use of a medium or a direct passage.
  • ～を通じて: Often implies a more passive or natural medium, and is slightly more formal when describing a duration.

4. Sentence Structure

[Noun] + を通じて / を通して
