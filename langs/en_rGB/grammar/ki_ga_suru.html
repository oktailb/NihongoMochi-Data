Vague Intuitions: ～気がする

Sometimes you don't have proof, but you just have a "feeling." ～気がする (written as ～気がする) is used to express an intuition, a hunch, or a vague impression. It's a very humble and natural way to state an opinion.

1. Sentence Structure

[Plain Form / Noun + na] + 気がする

2. Intuitive Examples

  • 今日はいいことがありそうな気がする。
    (I have a feeling that something good will happen today.)
  • 彼かれは本当ほんとうのことを言いっていない気がする。
    (I have a feeling that he isn't telling the truth.)
  • どこかで会あったことがある気がします。
    (I feel like I've met you somewhere before.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is much softer than ~to omou (I think). While ~to omou is a clear thought, ～気がする is a feeling that comes from your heart or your "ki" (energy). It's great for when you aren't 100% sure but want to share your vibe!
