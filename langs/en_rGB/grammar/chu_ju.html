Suffixes: ～中 (ちゅう / じゅう)

The kanji 中 can be read as chuu or juu depending on whether it describes an ongoing action or an entire period/space.

1. ～中 (ちゅうchuu): Ongoing Action

When added to a noun representing an action, it means "in the middle of" or "currently doing."

  • 仕事中しごとちゅう
    (In the middle of work / At work)
  • 授業中じゅぎょうちゅう
    (In the middle of class)
  • 電話中でんわちゅう
    (On the phone / Currently calling)

2. ～中 (じゅうjuu): Entire Period / Space

When added to a time or place, it means "all through" or "throughout the whole."

  • 一日中いちにちじゅう
    (All day long)
  • 一年中いちねんじゅう
    (All year round)
  • 世界中せかいじゅう
    (Throughout the whole world)

3. Summary Table

Reading Meaning Examples

じゅう (juu) All through / Throughout Day, Year, World, Town
