Hypothetical Resolve: たとえ～ても

Sometimes your determination is so strong that even a mountain won't stop you! たとえ～ても is used to say "even if X were to happen, the result (or my resolve) would not change." It sets up a strong hypothetical condition.

1. Sentence Structure

たとえ + [Verb/Adj Te-form] + も

2. Resolute Examples

  • たとえ反対はんたいされても、彼かれと結婚けっこんします。
    (Even if I am opposed, I will marry him.)
  • たとえ失敗しっぱいしても、後悔はしません。
    (Even if I fail, I will have no regrets.)
  • たとえ雨あめが降ふっても、サッカーの試合しあいはあります。
    (Even if it rains, there will be a soccer match.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is much stronger than a simple ~te mo. By adding たとえ at the beginning, you are emphasizing the "extreme" nature of the condition. It's the language of vows, deep promises, and unwavering logic. It says: "X doesn't matter; the outcome is fixed!"
