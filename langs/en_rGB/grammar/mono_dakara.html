Making an Excuse: ～ものだから / ～もんだから

When you've made a mistake or been late, you need a way to explain the reason softly. ～ものだから is used to state a cause or reason, especially when you are making an excuse or explaining something that was beyond your control.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb/Adj (Plain Form) + ものだから
  • Noun + な + ものだから
  • な-Adj + な + ものだから

2. Apologetic Examples

  • バスが遅おそれたものだから、遅刻ちこくしてしまいました。
    (Because the bus was late [as an excuse/explanation], I ended up being late.)
  • あまりにおいしいものだから、全部食べてしまった。
    (It was just so delicious that I ended up eating the whole thing.)
  • 急いそいでいたものだから、財布さいふを忘わすれてしまった。
    (Because I was in such a hurry, I accidentally left my wallet behind.)

3. Sensei's Note

In casual speech, it often becomes ～もんだから. It has a slightly "whiny" or "pleading" nuance, as if you are saying "It couldn't be helped because of X!" It's much more personal and subjective than node or kara. Use it when you want the listener to sympathize with your situation!
