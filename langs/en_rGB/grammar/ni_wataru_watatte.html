Extending Over: ～にわたって / ～にわたる

The expression ～にわたって (にわたって) is used to indicate that a situation or action extends over a specific period of time, frequency, or physical area. It translates to "throughout...", "over a period of...", or "extending over..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach にわたって directly to a Noun (time, distance, area, etc.). To modify a following noun, use にわたる.

[Noun] + にわたって

  • 会議かいぎは三時間さんじかんにわたって行おこなわれました。
    (The meeting was held over a period of three hours.)
  • 道路どうろは数すうキロにわたって渋滞じゅうたいしています。
    (The traffic jam extends over several kilometers of road.)
  • 二十年にじゅうねんにわたる研究けんきゅうがようやく終おわりました。
    (The research, which extended over twenty years, has finally finished.)

2. Key Usage: Large Scale

It is often used to emphasize the scale, length, or duration of something, making it sound more formal than simple particles like ~de or ~no aida.

3. Distinction from ～を通じて / ～を通して

  • ～を通じて: Focuses on the entire duration (e.g., "throughout the year").
  • ～にわたって: Focuses on the extent or scope of the action/state.
