Not Limited To: ～に限らず (にかぎらず)

When you want to say that something applies not just to one group or person, but to a wider range of things, we use ～に限らず. It means "not only... but also..." or "not limited to..."

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + に限らず

2. Inclusive Examples

  • このアニメあにめは子供こどもに限らず、大人おとなにも人気にんきがある。
    (This anime is popular not only with children, but also with adults.)
  • 日本語にほんごに限らず、どの言語げんごも勉強べ勉強するのは大変たいへんだ。
    (Not limited to Japanese, studying any language is difficult.)
  • 週末しゅうまつに限らず、この店みせはいつも込こんでいる。
    (Not just on weekends, this shop is always crowded.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is more formal than ~dake de naku. It highlights that the scope of a statement is much larger than just the specific example mentioned. It's a great way to talk about universal trends or broad topics!
