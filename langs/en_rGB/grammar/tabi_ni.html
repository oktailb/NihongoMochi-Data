Every Single Time: ～たびに

Some things in life are beautifully (or annoyingly) consistent! ～たびに (written as ～たびに) means "every time X happens, Y also happens." It highlights a repeating pattern or a constant reaction.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (Dictionary Form) + たびに
  • Noun + のたびに

2. Recurring Examples

  • この写真しゃしんを見みるたびに、家族かぞくを思おもい出だす。
    (Every time I look at this photo, I remember my family.)
  • 会あうたびに、彼かれはかっこよくなっている。
    (Every time we meet, he's getting more handsome.)
  • 旅たびのたびに、新あたらしい発見はっけんがある。
    (Every time I travel, there are new discoveries.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is stronger than itsumo. It connects two specific events. It says: "X is the trigger for Y." Use it to describe habits, emotions that are triggered by sights or sounds, or the inevitable growth that comes with time!
