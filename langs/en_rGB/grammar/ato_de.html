After Doing: ～あとで

To say "after doing something" or "after a certain event," Japanese uses ～あとで. It is the opposite of ～前まえに.

1. Using with Verbs

Use the Past Tense (Ta-form) of the verb. This is because the action must be completed before the next one starts.

[Verb た形たけい] + あとで

  • 仕事しごとが終おわったあとで、飲のみに行いきます。
    (After work finishes, I will go for a drink.)
  • 映画えいがを見みたあとで、カフェに行いきました。
    (After watching the movie, I went to a cafe.)

2. Using with Nouns

When using a noun (an event), connect it using the particle の.

[Noun] + の + あとで

  • 授業じゅぎょうのあとで、先生せんせいに質問しつもんしました。
    (After class, I asked the teacher a question.)
  • テストのあとで、アイスクリームを食たべました。
    (After the test, I ate ice cream.)

3. Comparison: ～てから

A similar structure is ～てから. While あとで simply marks the sequence of events, てから emphasizes that the first action is a prerequisite for the second.

  • 手てを洗あらってから、食たべます。
    (After washing [and because I washed] my hands, I eat.)
