The Potential Form (可能形かのうけい) - "Can do"

The Potential Form is used to express that someone has the ability to do something, or that a situation allows for an action to happen. It is more casual and direct than koto ga dekiru.

1. How to Conjugate

Group 1: U-Verbs (Godan)

Change the final "u" sound to the corresponding "e" sound and add る.

  • 行いく → 行いける (Can go)
  • 話はなす → 話はなせる (Can speak)
  • 飲のむ → 飲のめる (Can drink)
  • 待まつ → 待まてる (Can wait)

Group 2: Ru-Verbs (Ichidan)

Remove the final る and add られる.

  • 食たべる → 食たべられる (Can eat)
  • 見みる → 見みられる (Can see)

💡 Pro Tip: In casual speech, Japanese people often drop the "ra" and say 食たべれる (Ra-nuki kotoba).

Group 3: Irregular Verbs

  • する → できる (Can do)
  • 来くる → 来こられる (Can come)

2. The を to が Switch

When you use the potential form, the object of the action is usually marked with が instead of を.

  • 日本語にほんごを話はなします。(I speak Japanese.)
  • 日本語にほんごが話はなせます。(I can speak Japanese.)

3. Usage Note

Potential form verbs are always Group 2 (Ichidan) verbs. This means they conjugate like taberu for their past, negative, and polite forms.

  • 漢字かんじが書かけます (Polite)
  • 漢字かんじが書かけない (Negative)
