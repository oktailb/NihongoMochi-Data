In the middle of: ～最中に

The expression ～最中に (さいちゅうに) is used to describe something happening right in the middle of another action, often implying that the second action was an unexpected interruption.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach 最中に to the ~te iru form of a verb or to a Noun related to an event (using の).

  • 食事しょくじをしている最中に、電話でんわがかかってきました。
    (Right in the middle of eating, the phone rang.)
  • 会議かいぎの最中に、停電ていでんがありました。
    (There was a power outage in the middle of the meeting.)

2. Key Nuance: Interruption

Unlike ~aida ni (during), ～最中に emphasizes that the action was at its peak or that the interruption was particularly inconvenient or surprising.

  • スピーチの最中に、忘わすれてしまいました。
    (I forgot [my words] right in the middle of my speech.)

3. Important Note

The main action (the one that is interrupted) must be one that takes some time to complete (e.g., eating, meeting, talking, thinking). You cannot use it with instantaneous verbs like "waking up" or "dropping."
