Approximations: ごろ, ぐらい, and 約やく

In Japanese, there are different words for "about" or "approximately" depending on whether you are talking about a specific point in time or a duration/quantity.

1. ごろ (Points in Time)

Use ごろ (sometimes written as 頃) after a specific time or date to mean "around."

  • 三時さんじごろに帰かえります。
    (I will go home around 3 o'clock.)
  • 来年らいねんの一月いちがつごろ、日本にほんへ行いきます。
    (I will go to Japan around January of next year.)

2. ぐらい / くらい (Duration and Quantity)

Use ぐらい (or くらい) after a length of time or a quantity to mean "about" or "approximately."

  • 三時間さんじかんぐらい勉強べんきょうしました。
    (I studied for about three hours.)
  • りんごを五いつつぐらい買かいました。
    (I bought about five apples.)

3. 約やく (Formal Approximation)

約やく is a more formal way to say "approximately." Unlike the others, it is placed before the number.

  • 約やく三十分さんじゅっぷんかかります。
    (It takes approximately 30 minutes.)

4. Summary: ごろ vs ぐらい

Word Used for... Position
ごろ Specific points in time (3:00, Monday) After the noun
ぐらい Amounts, lengths of time, distances After the noun
