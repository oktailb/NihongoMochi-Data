No Way to...: ～ようがない

The expression ～ようがない (written as ～ようが無い) is used to say that there is absolutely no method or means to perform a certain action, even if one wanted to. It translates to "there is no way to...", "it's impossible to...", or "cannot possibly..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach ようがない to the Verb Stem (the part before ~masu).

[Verb Stem] + ようがない

  • かれの電話番号でんわばんごうがわからないので、連絡れんらくしようがない。
    (I don't know his phone number, so there is no way to contact him.)
  • こんなに壊こわれていたら、直なおしようがない。
    (If it's broken this badly, there is no way to fix it.)
  • あんなに怒おこっているかれには、何も言いいようがない。
    (There is no way to say anything to him while he's that angry.)

2. Key Nuance: Physical or Logical Impossibility

Unlike ～できない (general can't), ～ようがない specifically highlights the lack of a method or tool. It often implies a sense of powerlessness or that the situation is out of one's hands.
