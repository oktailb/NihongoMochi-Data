Unexpected Ratio: ～わりに

The expression ～わりに (or ～わには) is used when a result is different from what one would expect based on a certain standard or situation. It translates to "considering...", "for a...", or "relatively...".

1. Sentence Structure

Attach わりに to the Plain Form of verbs and adjectives. For nouns, use の.

  • この料理りょうりは、値段ねだんのわりにおいしいです。
    (Considering the price, this food is delicious [It's better than the price suggests].)
  • かれは年としのわりに若わかく見みえます。
    (He looks young for his age.)
  • あまり勉強べんきょうしなかったわりに、いい点てんがとれました。
    (Considering I didn't study much, I got a good grade.)

2. Connection Table

Type Connection
Verbs / い-Adj Plain Form + わりに
な-Adj Adj + な + わりに
Nouns Noun + の + わりに

3. Nuance: Subjective Surprise

Use わりに when you want to express that the actual state is surprising or impressive because it doesn't match the usual expectation for that category.
