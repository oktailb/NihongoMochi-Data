Tendency: ～ぎみ

The suffix ～ぎみ (written as ～気味) is used to express that someone or something is showing a slight tendency or sign of a certain state. It is usually used for negative or undesirable conditions. It translates to "a touch of...", "a bit...", or "seeming like..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach ぎみ to the Verb Stem or directly to a Noun.

[Verb Stem / Noun] + ぎみ

  • 風邪かぜぎみで、喉のどが痛いたいです。
    (I have a touch of a cold, and my throat hurts.)
  • 最近さいきん、太ふとりぎみなので、ダイエットをしています。
    (Lately, I've tended to gain a bit of weight, so I'm on a diet.)
  • 仕事しごとが遅おくれぎみです。
    (The work is starting to seem a bit late / lagging.)

2. Common Expressions

Expression Meaning
風邪かぜぎみ A touch of a cold
疲つかれぎみ A bit tired
不足ふそくぎみ A bit insufficient (e.g., lack of sleep)
緊張きんちょうぎみ A bit nervous

3. Nuance

Unlike ～がち (which describes a frequent habit), ～ぎみ describes a current state that is just starting to show or is slightly present.
