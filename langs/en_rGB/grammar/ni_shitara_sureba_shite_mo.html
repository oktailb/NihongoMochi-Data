From the perspective of: ～にしたら / ～にすれば / ～にしても

The expression ～にしたら (and its variants ～にすれば or ～にしても) is used to state an opinion or feeling from someone else's standpoint. It translates to "from the point of view of..." or "even for [someone]..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach these expressions directly to a Noun (usually a person or a group).

[Noun] + にしたら / にすれば / にしても

  • 親おやにしたら、子供こどもの将来しょうらいが一番いちばん心配しんぱいなのだ。
    (From the parents' point of view, their child's future is the biggest concern.)
  • かれにすれば、ただの冗談じょうだんだったのだろうが、私わたしは傷きずついた。
    (From his standpoint, it was probably just a joke, but I was hurt.)
  • プロの選手せんしゅにしても、この試合しあいは緊張きんちょうするだろう。
    (Even for a professional player, this match would be nerve-wracking.)

2. Key Nuance: Empathy or Supposition

It is often used when the speaker is trying to imagine or emphasize the feelings of someone else. ～にしても adds the nuance of "even from that person's standpoint."

3. Distinction from ～にとって

  • ～にとって: Expresses evaluation or importance (For me, this is difficult).
  • ～にしたら: Focuses more on the internal thoughts or feelings of the subject (If you were in their shoes...).
