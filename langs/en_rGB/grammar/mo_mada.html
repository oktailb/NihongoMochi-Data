Already and Not Yet: もう and まだ

These two adverbs are essential for talking about the status of an action or state in time.

1. もう (Already / Any more)

In affirmative sentences, it means "already." In negative sentences, it means "not any more."

  • もう昼ひるごはんにを食たべました。
    (I have already eaten lunch.)
  • もう雨あめは降ふっていません。
    (It is not raining any more.)

2. まだ (Still / Not yet)

In affirmative sentences, it means "still." In negative sentences, it means "not yet."

  • まだ雨あめが降ふっています。
    (It is still raining.)
  • いいえ、まだ食たべていません。
    (No, I have not eaten yet.)
