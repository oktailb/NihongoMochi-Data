Comparison: ～に比べて

The expression ～に比べて (にくらべて) is used to compare two things, indicating that one has a certain quality more or less than the other. It translates to "compared to..." or "in comparison with."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach に比べて directly to a Noun. You can also use に比べると with the same meaning.

[Noun] + に比べて

  • 去年きょねんに比べて、今年ことしは暑あついです。
    (Compared to last year, this year is hot.)
  • 兄あにに比べて、弟おとうとはよく勉強べんきょうします。
    (Compared to his older brother, the younger brother studies hard.)
  • バスは電車でんしゃに比べて時間じかんがかかります。
    (Buses take more time compared to trains.)

2. Formal Variation: ～に比べ

In formal writing or speeches, the て is often dropped.

  • 以前いぜんに比べ、生活せいかつが便利べんりになった。
    (Compared to before, life has become more convenient.)

3. Distinction from ～より

  • ～より: A simple comparison ("A is more than B").
  • ～に比べて: Specifically emphasizes the act of comparing two distinct things or situations.
