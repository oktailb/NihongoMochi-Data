On the eve of / At the start of: ～にあたって / ～にあたり

The expression ～にあたって (にあたって) is used to indicate a special time or event that marks a new stage or an important turning point. It translates to "on the occasion of...", "at the start of...", or "in preparation for..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach にあたって directly to a Noun or the Dictionary Form of a verb. The version にあたり is more formal and often used in writing or formal speeches.

[Noun / Verb Dictionary Form] + にあたって

  • 新年しんねんを迎むかえるにあたって、目標もくひょうを決きめました。
    (On the occasion of welcoming the New Year, I decided on my goals.)
  • 新あたらしいプロジェクトを始はじめるにあたり、いくつかの課題かだいがあります。
    (Upon starting the new project, there are several issues.)
  • 結婚けっこんにあたって、両親りょうしんに感謝かんしゃの言葉ことばを伝つたえた。
    (On the occasion of my marriage, I expressed my gratitude to my parents.)

2. Key Nuance: Positive Preparation

It is almost always used for positive, intentional, or formal events. It implies a sense of readiness or looking forward to a new beginning. You wouldn't use it for accidents or negative incidents.

3. Distinction from ～に際して

  • ～に際して: Neutral point-in-time focus (Upon the time of...).
  • ～にあたって: Focuses on the "facing" of an event and the preparations or feelings associated with starting it.
