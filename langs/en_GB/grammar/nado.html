Enumeration: ～など

The particle `など` is used when listing examples. It translates to "and so on," "etcetera," or "among others." It implies that the list is not exhaustive.

1. Sentence Structure

It is often used in combination with the particle `や`.

`[Noun A] や [Noun B] など`

  • 机つくえの上うえに本ほん`や`ペン`など`があります。
    (There are books, pens, `and so on` on the desk.)
  • スーパーで肉にく`や`野菜やさい`など`を買かいました。
    (I bought meat, vegetables, `etc.` at the supermarket.)

2. Difference from ～と

  • `～と:` Lists `all` items (exhaustive).
  • `～や ... など:` Lists only a few `representative` items.

3. Usage with Verbs

While less common at the N5 level, it can also follow a verb in its dictionary form to give examples of actions.

  • 休やすみの日ひは掃除そうじをしたり、洗濯せんたくをしたり`など`します。
    (On holidays, I do things `like` cleaning and doing laundry.)
