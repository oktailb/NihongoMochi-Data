Never Again: 二度と (にどと)

When you have made a firm decision or experienced something so terrible that you never want it to happen again, we use `二度と (にどと)`. It is always paired with a negative verb to express "never again."

1. Sentence Structure

`二度と` + Verb (Negative Form)

2. Firm Examples

  • あんな所ところへは`二度と行いかない`。
    (I will never go to a place like that again!)
  • `二度と嘘うそをつかない`と約束やくそくします。
    (I promise that I will never tell a lie again.)
  • こんな失敗しっぱいは`二度と起おこしたくない`。
    (I never want to make a mistake like this again.)

3. Sensei's Note

This word is very powerful. Use it when you want to show your iron will or a very strong emotional boundary. If you just mean "not often," use `あまり　ない`. `二度と` is for those life-changing "never again" moments!
