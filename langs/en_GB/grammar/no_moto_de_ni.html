Under the Influence: ～の下で / ～の下に (のもとで / のもとに)

How we grow often depends on who is guiding us or what environment we are in. ～の下で (written as ～の下で) means "under the guidance of" or "under the conditions of." It describes the influence or environment surrounding an action.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + の下で / の下に / のもとで / のもとに

2. Influential Examples

  • 厳きびしい両親りょうしんの下で育そだちました。
    (I was raised under strict parents.)
  • 優秀ゆうしゅうなコーチこーちの下で練習れんしゅうに励はげむ。
    (To strive in practice under an excellent coach.)
  • 平等びょうどうの名の下に、裁判さいばんが行われる。
    (In the name of equality, the trial is conducted.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of this as a protective (or restrictive) "roof" over an activity. ～の下で is usually for people and environments, while ～の下に often refers to abstract principles or conditions (like "under the name of..."). It's a very respectful and literary way to show connection!
