"After doing...": ～上で

The expression ～上で (うえで) is used to say that an action is performed only after another action is completed, usually as a necessary preparation or basis for a decision. It translates to "after...", "upon...", or "on the basis of..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach 上で to the Past Form (Ta-form) of a verb or to a Noun (using の).

[Verb た形たけい] + 上で

  • 内容ないようをよく確認かくにんした上で、サインしてください。
    (Please sign after confirming the contents thoroughly.)
  • 家族かぞくと相談そうだんした上で、お返事へんじします。
    (I will give you an answer after consulting with my family.)
  • 実物じつぶつを見みた上で、買かうかどうか決きめます。
    (I will decide whether to buy it after seeing the actual item.)

2. Key Nuance: Prerequisite for Action

Unlike simple ~ato de (after), ～上で implies that the first action is a deliberate step required before the second action can be taken. It is very common in formal business or legal contexts.

3. Distinction from ～てから

  • ～てから: Simply describes the order of two actions.
  • ～上で: Emphasizes that the first action provides the foundation or information needed for the second action.
