Thanks to: ～おかげで

The expression `～おかげで` is used to express gratitude for a `positive result`. It translates to "thanks to..." or "owing to..."

1. Sentence Structure

It connects to verbs, adjectives, and nouns in their plain forms.

  • `Verbs:` 薬くすりを飲のんだ`おかげで`、元気げんきになりました。
    (Thanks to taking the medicine, I got well.)
  • `Nouns:` 先生せんせい`のおかげで`、合格ごうかくしました。
    (Thanks to the teacher, I passed.)
  • `Adj:` 天気てんきが良よかった`おかげで`、景色けしきがきれいに見みえました。
    (Thanks to the good weather, the view looked beautiful.)

2. Connection Table

Type Connection
Verbs / い-Adj Plain Form + おかげで
な-Adj Adj + `な` + おかげで
Nouns Noun + `の` + おかげで

3. Important Nuance

Never use `おかげで` for negative situations unless you are being highly sarcastic. For negative results, use `せいで` instead.
