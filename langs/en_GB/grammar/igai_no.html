Except and Beyond: ～以外 (いがい)

When you want to exclude something from a group or talk about things "other than" a specific item, we use `～以外 (いがい)`. It is essential for defining boundaries and exceptions.

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Noun` + 以外 (の + Noun)
  • `Verb (Plain Form)` + 以外

2. Defining Examples

  • 彼かれ`以外`はみんな来くました。
    (Everyone came except for him.)
  • この仕事しごと`以外`に何なにかできることはありますか？
    (Is there anything else I can do besides this job?)
  • 勉強べんきょうする`以外`に方法ほうほうはない。
    (There is no way other than to study.)

3. Sensei's Note

`以外` is very precise. If you want to say "In addition to X, there is also Y," you might use `ほか　に`. But if you want to strictly draw a circle around X and say "everything outside this circle," `以外` is your best tool!
