Despite (Critical): ～くせに

The expression ～くせに is used to say "even though" or "despite," but with a strong nuance of criticism, contempt, or irritation. It is used when someone does something that contradicts their status or what they previously said.

1. Sentence Structure

It connects to the Plain Form of verbs and adjectives. For nouns, add の.

  • かれは自分じぶんで何もできないくせに、いつも文句もんくを言いう。
    (Despite not being able to do anything himself, he's always complaining.)
  • 知しっているくせに、教おしえてくれません。
    (Even though he knows [the answer], he won't tell me.)
  • 子供こどものくせに、そんなことを言いってはいけません。
    (Even though you're just a child, you shouldn't say things like that.)

2. Connection Table

Type Connection
Verbs / い-Adj Plain Form + くせに
な-Adj Adj + な + くせに
Nouns Noun + の + くせに

3. Important Usage Note

Because of its critical and sometimes insulting nuance, never use this to talk about your superiors or in formal situations. It is strictly for casual speech where you want to express disapproval.
