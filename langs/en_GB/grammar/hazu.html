Expectation: ～はず

The structure `～はず` is used to express an expectation based on objective reasons or logical deduction. It means "it is expected that..." or "it should be the case that..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `はず` to the `Plain Form` of verbs and adjectives. For nouns, use `の`.

  • `Verbs:` 来くる`はず`です (He is expected to come / He should come).
  • `い-Adj:` 美味おいしい`はず`です (It should be delicious).
  • `な-Adj:` 暇ひま`なはず`です (He should be free).
  • `Nouns:` 今日きょう`のはず`です (It should be today).

2. Key Examples

  • 田中たなかさんは今日きょう、来くる`はず`です。
    (Mr. Tanaka is supposed to come today [e.g., because he said so].)
  • この薬くすりを飲のめば、良よくなる`はず`です。
    (If you take this medicine, you should get better.)

3. Negative Expectation: ～はずがない

To say something is "impossible" or "cannot be the case," use `はずがない`.

  • そんな`はずがありません`！
    (That cannot be possible!)
  • あの真面目まじめな田中たなかさんが嘘うそをつく`はずがありません`。
    (It's impossible that the serious Mr. Tanaka would tell a lie.)

4. Nuance Note

`～はず` implies that you have a specific reason for your belief. If you are just guessing without a strong reason, use `～でしょう` or `～かもしれない` instead.
