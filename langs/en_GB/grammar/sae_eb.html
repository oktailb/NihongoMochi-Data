The Only Thing You Need: ～さえ～ば

Sometimes, all you need is one single thing to go right! `～さえ～ば` is used to express the "minimum condition." It means "If only X happens, then everything else will be fine." It narrows down all requirements to just one essential piece.

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Noun` + さえ + [Verb-eba / Adj-kereba / Adj-nara]
  • `Verb Stem` + さえ + すれば

2. Minimalist Examples

  • 薬くすりを飲のみ`さえすれば`、病気びょうきは治なおります。
    (If you only take the medicine, the illness will be cured.)
  • お金かね`さえあれば`、何でもできると思おもうのは間違いだ。
    (It's a mistake to think that if you only have money, you can do anything.)
  • あなたが元気げんきでいて`さえくれれば`、私はうれしい。
    (As long as you are healthy/well, I am happy.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very powerful "if." It tells the listener that Factor X is the "key" to the whole situation. It's often used when expressing wishes or giving advice on what's most important. "If only this one thing is okay, the rest doesn't matter!"
