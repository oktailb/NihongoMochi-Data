"Even while...": ～ながらも

The expression ～ながらも is used to show a contrast between two states or actions. It translates to "even though," "while... still," or "despite." It is a more formal and emphatic version of the contrastive nagara.

1. Sentence Structure

It can be attached to the Verb Stem, Adjectives, or Nouns.

  • 狭せまいながらも、楽たのしい我わが家やです。
    (Even though it's small, it's my happy home.)
  • 残念ざんねんながらも、その提案ていあんは受うけ入いれられません。
    (While it is regrettable, we cannot accept that proposal.)
  • 下手へたながらも、一生懸命いっしょうけんめいに練習れんしゅうしています。
    (Even though I'm not good at it, I'm practicing with all my might.)

3. Distinction from ～ながら

While ～ながら is often used for simultaneous actions (while walking...), ～ながらも specifically emphasizes the contradiction or contrast between the two parts of the sentence.
