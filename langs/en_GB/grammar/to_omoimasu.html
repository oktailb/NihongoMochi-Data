Expressing Opinions and Thoughts: ～と思おもいます

To express your opinion, a guess, or what you are thinking, Japanese uses the particle `と` followed by the verb `思おomいます` (to think).

1. Sentence Structure

The sentence before `と思おもいます` must always be in its `Plain Form` (Casual form), even if the overall conversation is polite.

`[Plain Form Sentence] + と思おもいます`

With Verbs:

  • 明日あしたは雨あめが降ふる`と思おもいます`。
    (I think it will rain tomorrow.)
  • 田中さんはもう帰かえった`と思おもいます`。
    (I think Mr. Tanaka already went home.)

With Adjectives:

  • この本ほんはおもしろい`と思おもいます`。
    (I think this book is interesting.)
  • この町まちは静しずかだ`と思おもいます`。
    (I think this town is quiet.)

2. Asking for Opinions

Use `どう思おもいますか` to ask someone else's opinion.

  • 日本について`どう思おもいますか`。
    (What do you think about Japan?)

3. Important Grammar Note

Remember that for `Nouns` and `Na-adjectives` in the present tense, you must add `だ` before the particle `と`.

  • 明日あしたは休やすみ`だ`と思おもいます。
    (I think tomorrow is a holiday.)
