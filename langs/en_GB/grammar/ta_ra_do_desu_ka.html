Making Suggestions: ～たらどうですか

The expression `～たらどうですか` is used to make a suggestion or give advice to someone. It translates to "How about...?" or "Why don't you...?"

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `どうですか` to the `~たら` conditional form of a verb.

`[Verb ～たら] + どうですか`

  • もっと早はやく`寝ねたらどうですか`。
    (How about going to bed earlier?)
  • わからないときは、先生せんせいに`聞きいたらどうですか`。
    (Why don't you ask the teacher when you don't understand?)
  • ちょっと`休やすんだらどうですか`。
    (How about taking a little rest?)

2. Nuance and Politeness

While grammatically polite, this expression can sometimes sound like you are telling someone what to do. If you want to be even softer, you can use `～たらどうでしょうか` or just `～てみたら？` (casual).

3. Casual Version

In casual speech with friends, you can just say `～たら？` with a rising intonation.

  • もっと勉強べんきょうしたら？ (Why don't you study more? [Casual])
