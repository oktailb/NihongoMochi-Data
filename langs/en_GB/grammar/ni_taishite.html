Towards / In Contrast to: ～に対して

The expression `～に対して` (にたいして) has two main meanings: it can indicate an action or attitude directed toward someone, or it can show a contrast between two things.

1. Attitude or Action Toward Someone

Use this when you are doing something `to` or `toward` a person or group.

  • 目上めうえの人ひと`に対して`、敬語けいごを使つかいます。
    (We use Keigo toward people of higher status.)
  • 客きゃく`に対して`、失礼しつれいな態度たいどをとってはいけません。
    (You must not have a rude attitude toward customers.)

2. Contrast between Two Things

It translates to "in contrast to" or "whereas." In this case, it often connects to a clause using `の`.

  • 兄あにが活発かっぱつな`のに対して`、弟おとうとは静しずかだ。
    (In contrast to my older brother being active, my younger brother is quiet.)
  • 都市部としぶは便利べんりな`に対して`、農村部のうそんぶは不便ふべんだ。
    (Whereas urban areas are convenient, rural areas are inconvenient.)
