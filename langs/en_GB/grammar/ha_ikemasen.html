Prohibition: ～てはいけません

The expression ～てはいけません is used to say that something is forbidden or that you must not do something. It is the formal way of saying "don't" or "you are not allowed to."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach はいけません to the Te-form of a verb.

[Verb て形てけい] + はいけません

  • ここで写真しゃしんを撮とってはいけません。
    (You must not take photos here.)
  • お酒さけを飲のんではいけません。
    (You are not allowed to drink alcohol.)

2. Casual Version

In casual speech with friends, the "wa" is often omitted, or "wa" becomes a "cha" or "ja" sound.

  • ～ては → ～ちゃ (e.g., 行いっちゃだめ)
  • ～では → ～じゃ (e.g., 飲んじゃだめ)

3. Connection with Permission

This is the opposite of ～てもいいです (You may...). Together, they are used to set rules and boundaries.
