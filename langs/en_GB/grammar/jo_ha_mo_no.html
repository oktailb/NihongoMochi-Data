Perspectives and Domains: ～上（は・も） / ～上の (じょう)

When you want to look at a situation from a specific angle—like health, law, or history—we use `～上 (じょう)`. It means "from the standpoint of," "in terms of," or "in the domain of." It narrows down the context of your statement.

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Noun` + 上（は・も） (As an adverb)
  • `Noun` + 上の + `Noun` (As an adjective)

2. Domain Examples

  • 健康けんこう`上`の理由りゆうで、会社かいしゃを辞やめました。
    (I quit the company for health reasons [reasons in terms of health].)
  • 法律ほうりつ`上`は問題もんだいありません。
    (From a legal standpoint, there are no problems.)
  • 歴史れきし`上`、非常ひじょうに重要じゅうような出来事できごとだ。
    (Historically [In terms of history], it's a very important event.)

3. Sensei's Note

This suffix is extremely useful for professional and academic Japanese. It allows you to specify exactly which "box" you are talking about. Note that it's read as `じょう` here, not `うえ`. Use it when you want to sound precise and analytical!
