"As soon as": ～次第

The conjunction `～次第` (しだい) is a formal way to say "as soon as" or "immediately after something happens." It is commonly used in business or formal announcements.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `次第` to the `Verb Stem`. Unlike `~たら`, it implies that once the first action is finished, the next action will happen `immediately` and intentionally.

`[Verb Stem] + 次第`

  • 準備じゅんびが`でき次第`、出発しゅっぱつします。
    (We will depart `as soon as` preparations are complete.)
  • 事務所じむしょに`つき次第`、お電話でんわします。
    (I will call you `as soon as` I arrive at the office.)

2. Important Restriction

The sentence following `次第` usually expresses a future intention or request. You `cannot` use it for past events or natural, uncontrollable consequences.

3. Other Meanings (N2)

In more advanced contexts, `次第` can also mean "depending on" when used with a noun.

  • 努力どりょく`次第`で、何なにでもできる。
    (`Depending on` your effort, you can do anything.)
