Simultaneous Actions: ～ながら

The conjunction `～ながら` is used to describe two actions happening at the same time by the same person. It translates to "while doing" or "as."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `ながら` to the `Verb Stem` (the part before `~ます`).

`[Verb Stem] + ながら、[Main Verb]`

  • 音楽おんがくを`聞ききながら`、勉強べんきょうします。
    (I study `while listening` to music.)
  • テレビを`見みながら`、食事しょくじをします。
    (I eat `while watching` TV.)
  • 歩ある`きながら`、話はなしましょう。
    (Let's talk `while walking`.)

2. The Main Focus

In a `～ながら` sentence, the second verb is always the `primary action`. The first verb is the background or secondary action.

  • 働はたらきながら、大学だいがくに通かよっています。
    (While working [secondary], I am attending university [primary].)

3. Usage with Adjectives and Nouns (N3/N2)

In more advanced Japanese, `ながら` can also mean "although" or "despite" when used with adjectives or nouns.

  • 残念ざんねん`ながら`、行いけません。
    (`Although` it's a pity, I cannot go.)
