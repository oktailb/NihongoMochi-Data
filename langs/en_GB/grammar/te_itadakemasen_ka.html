Polite Requests: ～ていただけませんか

In Japanese culture, asking for a favor is an art form. `～ていただけませんか` is a highly polite and humble way to make a request. It literally translates to "Would you not be able to do... for me?" and shows great respect for the other person's time and status.

1. Sentence Structure

`[Verb Te-form] + いただけませんか / いただけないでしょうか`

2. Respectful Examples

  • この漢字かんじの読よみ方かたを`教おしえていただけませんか`。
    (Could you please teach me how to read this Kanji?)
  • 少すこし`手伝てつだっていただけませんか`。
    (Would you mind helping me for a moment?)
  • 荷物にもつを`預あずかっていただけないでしょうか`。
    (I wonder if you could possibly look after my luggage?)

3. Sensei's Note

This is much more polite than `~て　ください` (which is actually a mild command). Use `～ていただけませんか` when speaking to teachers, superiors, or strangers. It gives the other person a "way out" to say no politely, which ironically makes them more likely to want to help you!
