The Logical Conditional: ～ば

The ～ば form (often called the "conditional form") focuses on the condition itself. It is used to express that if a specific condition is met, a certain result will follow.

1. How to Conjugate

Group 1: U-Verbs (Godan)

Change the final "u" sound to the corresponding "e" sound and add ば.

  • 行いく → 行いけば (If [one] goes)
  • 話はなす → 話はなせば (If [one] speaks)
  • 飲のむ → 飲のめば (If [one] drinks)

Group 2: Ru-Verbs (Ichidan)

Replace the final る with れば.

  • 食たべる → 食たべれば (If [one] eats)
  • 見みる → 見みれば (If [one] sees)

Group 3: Irregular Verbs

  • する → すれば
  • 来くる → 来くれば (pronounced ku-reba)

Adjectives

  • い-Adjectives: Replace the final ～い with ～ければ.
    安やすい → 安やすければ (If it is cheap)

2. Common Usage

  1. General Truths and Proverbs:
    雨あめが降ふれば、地面じめんが濡ぬれます。
    (If it rains, the ground gets wet.)
  2. Giving Advice or Recommendations:
    どうすればいいですか。
    (What should I do? / lit: If I do what, is it good?)
  3. "The more... the more...":
    勉強べんきょうすればするほど、わかります。
    (The more you study, the more you understand.)

3. Important Note

Similar to ～と, you generally cannot use a command or request in the second part of the sentence if the first part is an action. However, if the first part is a state (like an adjective or the verb aru), a request is allowed!
