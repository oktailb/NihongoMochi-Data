"After long...": ～すえに / ～のすえ

The expression `～すえに` (written as `～末に`) is used to describe a final outcome that is reached after a long and often difficult process or period of time. Unlike `~あげく`, the result can be either positive or negative.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `すえに` to the `Past Form (Ta-form)` of a verb or to a `Noun` (using `の`).

`[Verb た形たけい / Noun の] + すえに`

  • いろいろ考かんがえた`すえに`、留学りゅうがくすることに決きめました。
    (After thinking it over for a long time, I finally decided to study abroad.)
  • 苦労くろうの`すえに`、ついに自分じぶんの店みせを持もつことができた。
    (After much hardship, I was finally able to have my own shop.)
  • 激はげしい議論ぎろんの`すえ`、新あたらしい方針ほうしんが決きまった。
    (After intense discussion, a new policy was decided.)

2. Key Nuance: Culmination

It emphasizes that the result is the "end point" or the "sum total" of everything that happened before. It often implies a significant effort or a long duration of time (weeks, months, or years).

3. Summary Comparison

Grammar Common Result Nuance
`～あげく` Negative Sense of "useless effort" or frustration.
`～すえに` Pos. or Neg. Sense of a long-awaited final outcome.
