"So... that...": ～あまり

The expression `～あまり` (written as `～余り`) is used when a feeling or a state is so extreme that it causes an unexpected or unusual result. It translates to "because of too much...", "so... that...", or "in an excess of..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `あまり` to `Nouns` (using `の`) or to the `Plain Form` of verbs (often in the `Ta-form` or Dictionary form).

`[Noun の / Verb Plain] + あまり`

  • 嬉うれしさの`あまり`、涙なみだが出ました。
    (I was `so happy that` tears came out.)
  • 緊張きんちょうする`あまり`、言いいたいことを忘わすれてしまった。
    (Because I was `too nervous`, I forgot what I wanted to say.)
  • 忙いそがしさの`あまり`、食事しょくじをするのも忘わすれていた。
    (In an `excess of busyness`, I even forgot to eat.)

2. Key Nuance: Uncontrollable Result

It usually describes a result that the person could not control or did not intend, caused by an overwhelming emotion or situation. The result is often something slightly negative or surprising.

3. Common Nouns used with ～あまり

Feeling With あまり Meaning
驚おどろき 驚おどろきのあまり Out of surprise
悲かなしみ 悲かなしみのあまり Out of sadness
怒いかり 怒いかりのあまり Out of anger
