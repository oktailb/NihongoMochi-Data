Following Instructions: ～とおりに and ～どおりに

The expression `～とおりに` is used to say that an action is performed exactly as something else indicates—following a path, a set of instructions, or a thought. It translates to "exactly as...", "following...", or "according to...".

1. Sentence Structure

The connection depends on whether you use a verb or a noun.

With Verbs (Dictionary or Ta-form)

  • 私わたしが言いった`とおりに`してください。
    (Please do `exactly as` I said.)
  • 説明書せつめいしょに書かいてある`とおりに`、組くみ立たてました。
    (I assembled it `exactly as` written in the manual.)

With Nouns

You can use `Noun + の + とおり` or the voiced version `Noun + どおり` (without the particle `の`).

  • 地図ちず`のとおりに`行いけば、着つきます。
    (If you go `according to` the map, you will arrive.)
  • 計画けいかく`どおりに`進すすんでいます。
    (It is proceeding `according to` plan.)

2. Connection Table

Type Connection
Verbs Plain Form + `とおりに`
Nouns (Method 1) Noun + `の` + `とおりに`
Nouns (Method 2) Noun + `どおりに`

3. Common Expressions

  • 思おもったとおり (Exactly as I thought)
  • 期待きたいどおり (As expected)
  • 命令めいれいどおり (As ordered)
