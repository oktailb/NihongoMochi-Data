Appearance and Hearsay: ～そう

The expression `～そう` has two completely different meanings depending on how you connect it. It can mean "it looks like" (appearance) or "I heard that" (hearsay).

1. Appearance: "It looks like..."

Use this when you make a guess based on what you see. Connect it to the `Verb Stem` or `Adjective Stem`.

  • 雨あめが`降ふりそう`です。
    (It looks like it will rain.)
  • この料理りょうりは`おいしそう`です。
    (This food looks delicious.)
  • かれは`暇ひまそう`です。
    (He looks like he is free.)

`⚠️ Exception:` The adjective `いい` (good) becomes `よさそう`.

2. Hearsay: "I heard that..."

Use this to report information from another source. Connect it to the `Plain Form` (Casual form) of the sentence.

  • 明日あしたは雨あめが`降ふるそう`です。
    (I heard that it will rain tomorrow.)
  • この料理りょうりは`おいしいそう`です。
    (I heard that this food is delicious.)

3. Summary of Connection

Meaning Connection Example (降ふる)
`Appearance` (Looks like) Stem Form 降ふりそう
`Hearsay` (I heard) Plain Form 降ふるそう
