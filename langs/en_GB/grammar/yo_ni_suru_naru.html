Changes and Efforts: ～ようになる and ～ようにする

These two expressions describe changes in habits or abilities, and the conscious efforts we make to achieve them.

1. Change of State: ～ようになる

This is used to describe a change that has happened over time, such as a new habit or a newly acquired ability. It translates to "to reach the point where..." or "to come to..."

`[Verb Dictionary / Potential / Nai Form] + ようになる`

  • 日本にほんの新聞しんぶんが読よめる`ようになりました`。
    (I've reached the point where I can read Japanese newspapers.)
  • 最近さいきん、お酒さけを飲のまない`ようになりました`。
    (Lately, I've come to not drink alcohol [I stopped the habit].)

2. Making an Effort: ～ようにする

This is used to describe a conscious effort to establish a habit or to make sure something happens. It translates to "to try to..." or "to make an effort to..."

`[Verb Dictionary / Nai Form] + ようにする`

  • 毎日まいにち、野菜やさいを食たべる`ようにしています`。
    (I am making an effort to eat vegetables every day.)
  • 忘わすれない`ようにしてください`。
    (Please try not to forget / Make sure not to forget.)

3. The Difference

  • `～ようになる:` Focuses on the `result` of a change (it just happened or was achieved).
  • `～ようにする:` Focuses on the `process` and the will of the person (I am trying to make it happen).
