Far From It!: ～どころではない / ～どころか

When you want to emphasize that a situation is completely different from what was expected—or even worse—we use ～どころではない or ～どころか. It's like saying "Forget about X, it's actually Y!"

1. Sentence Structure

  • Noun + どころではない / どころか
  • Verb (Plain Form) + どころではない / どころか

2. Dramatic Examples

  • 風邪かぜがひどくて、遊あそびに行くどころではない。
    (My cold is so bad, going out to play is out of the question!)
  • 漢字かんじが書かけないどころか、名前なまえも読よめない。
    (Far from not being able to write Kanji, I can't even read the name!)
  • 忙いそがしくて、昼ひるごはんを食たべる時間じかんどころではない。
    (I'm so busy, eating lunch is the last thing on my mind.)

3. Sensei's Note

～どころではない emphasizes that the current state or situation makes doing something impossible. ～どころか is used to show a huge gap between an assumption and the reality (often a much worse reality). Use these when you want to sound emphatic!
