Both This and That: ～も～ば～も

Life isn't just one thing or another; sometimes it's both! This pattern is used to emphasize that multiple qualities (often both good or both bad) exist at the same time. It means "There is X, and there is also Y."

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Noun` + も + [Verb-eba / Adj-kereba / Adj-nara] + `Noun` + も

2. Abundant Examples

  • この町まちは山やま`もあれば`海うみ`もある`。
    (This town has both mountains and the sea.)
  • 人生じんせいには楽たのしい時とき`もあれば`、辛つらい時とき`もある`。
    (In life, there are happy times, and there are also painful times.)
  • 彼かれは頭あたま`もよければ`性格せいかく`もいい`。
    (He is both smart and has a good personality.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very rhythmic and balanced way to speak. It shows that you are looking at the whole picture. It's often used to describe a person's talents or the diverse features of a place. It makes your Japanese sound very polished and observant!
