Obvious Certainty: ～に決まっている

When something is so obvious that there's no room for doubt, we use ～に決まっている. It expresses the speaker's strong conviction that something must be true. It's like saying "Of course!" or "It's bound to be...!"

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb/Adj/Noun Plain Form] + に決まっている

2. Sure-fire Examples

  • そんなの、嘘うそに決まっている！
    (That's bound to be a lie! / Of course that's a lie!)
  • 一生懸命いっしょうけんめい練習れんしゅうしたんだから、勝かつに決まっている。
    (Since we practiced so hard, we're bound to win.)
  • そんなことをしたら、怒おこられるに決まっている。
    (If you do such a thing, you're bound to get scolded.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a subjective but very strong statement. It shows you're 100% sure based on your own logic or feelings. In casual speech, it often becomes ～にきまってる. Use it when the conclusion seems like common sense to you!
