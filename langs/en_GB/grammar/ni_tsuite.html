Defining the Topic: ～について

This is one of the most fundamental tools in your kit! `～について` is used to introduce the topic or subject matter you are discussing, studying, or thinking about. it means "about" or "concerning."

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + について (の + Noun)

2. Topical Examples

  • 日本にほんの文化ぶんか`について`勉強べんきょうしています。
    (I am studying about Japanese culture.)
  • その件けん`について`は、後で話はなしましょう。
    (Concerning that matter, let's talk about it later.)
  • 自分じぶんの将来しょうらい`についての`作文さくぶんを書かく。
    (To write an essay about my own future.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is the standard, neutral way to state your theme. Whether you're making a speech or just chatting, `～について` acts as a signpost, telling your listener exactly what "territory" your words are covering. Use it to be clear and organized!
