"Now that...": ～以上（は）

The expression `～以上（は）` (いじょうは) is used to express a strong sense of responsibility or determination. It translates to "now that...", "since...", or "so long as...". It implies that because condition A is true, it is only natural or required that B should follow.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `以上（は）` to the `Plain Form` of verbs and adjectives (often in the `Ta-form` for past facts). For nouns, use `である`.

`[Verb Plain / Noun である] + 以上（は）`

  • 約束やくそくした`以上は`、守まもらなければなりません。
    (Now that you've made a promise, you must keep it.)
  • 試験しけんを受うける`以上`、いい点てんを取とりたいです。
    (Since I am taking the exam, I want to get a good grade.)
  • 学生がくせいである`以上`、勉強べんきょうするのが当あたり前だ。
    (As long as [you] are a student, studying is only natural.)

2. Key Nuance: Duty and Resolve

This expression is often followed by words expressing obligation, determination, or advice (like `~なければ　ならない`, `~べき　だ`, or `~つもり　だ`). it carries a heavy tone of "no excuses."

3. Distinction from ～からには

Both mean "since/now that," but `～以上は` is more formal and focuses on the objective fact or status, while `～からには` often emphasizes the speaker's strong personal resolve.
