Finishing a Task: ～おわる

While ~nuku is about the struggle, ～おわる (written as ～終わる) is your everyday reliable friend for simply finishing an action. It marks the completion of a process.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach おわる to the Verb Stem.

[Verb Stem] + おわる

2. Clear Examples

  • この本ほんを読よみおわったら、かしてください。
    (When you finish reading this book, please lend it to me.)
  • 晩ばんごはんを食たべおわりました。
    (I have finished eating dinner.)
  • ようやくレポートを書かきおわった！
    (I finally finished writing the report!)

3. Sensei's Note

Use ～おわる for neutral completion. It is the most common way to say you're done with something. Compare it to ～はじめる (to start doing), and you'll see how useful these verb-verb combinations are!
