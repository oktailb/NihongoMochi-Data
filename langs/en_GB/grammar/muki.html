Suitable for the Task: ～向き (むき)

While `~むけ` is about "intention," `～向き` is about "suitability." It means that something is naturally a good fit for a certain person or purpose, regardless of whether it was originally designed for them or not. It means "suitable for" or "appropriate for."

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + 向き (だ / に / の + Noun)

2. Appropriate Examples

  • この仕事しごとは彼かれ`向き`だ。
    (This job is a good fit for him / He is suitable for this work.)
  • この服ふくは夏なつ`向き`の素材そざいでできている。
    (This clothing is made of material suitable for summer.)
  • ここは初心者しょしんしゃ`向き`のコースです。
    (This is a course appropriate for beginners.)

3. Sensei's Note

Remember: `向け` = "I made this for you." `向き` = "This happens to be perfect for you." For example, a "kids' menu" is `こども-むけ`, but a small, light chair might be `こども-むき` because it's the right size for them!
