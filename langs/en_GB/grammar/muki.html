Suitable for the Task: ～向き (むき)

While ~muke is about "intention," ～向き is about "suitability." It means that something is naturally a good fit for a certain person or purpose, regardless of whether it was originally designed for them or not. It means "suitable for" or "appropriate for."

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + 向き (だ / に / の + Noun)

2. Appropriate Examples

  • この仕事しごとは彼かれ向きだ。
    (This job is a good fit for him / He is suitable for this work.)
  • この服ふくは夏なつ向きの素材そざいでできている。
    (This clothing is made of material suitable for summer.)
  • ここは初心者しょしんしゃ向きのコースです。
    (This is a course appropriate for beginners.)

3. Sensei's Note

Remember: 向け = "I made this for you." 向き = "This happens to be perfect for you." For example, a "kids' menu" is kodomo-muke, but a small, light chair might be kodomo-muki because it's the right size for them!
