Finish completely: ～きれる / ～きれない

The suffix `～きる` (to finish completely) becomes `～きれる` in its potential form. It is used to express that an action can or cannot be finished to the very end due to its volume or difficulty.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `きれる` or `きれない` to the `Verb Stem`.

  • こんなにたくさんの料理りょうりは、一人ひとりでは`食たべきれません`。
    (I cannot finish eating all this food by myself.)
  • かれは一晩ひとばんでその本ほんを`読よみきった`。
    (He finished reading the entire book in one night.)
  • 数かずえ`きれない`ほどたくさんの星ほしが見みえます。
    (I can see so many stars that they are uncountable [cannot finish counting].)

2. Common Verbs

Verb Meaning
使つかいきる To use up completely
走はしいきる To run the whole distance
信しんじきる To believe completely / without doubt
