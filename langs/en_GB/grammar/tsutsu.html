Simultaneous and Ongoing Actions: ～つつ

The conjunction `～つつ` is a more formal and literary version of `～ながら`. It is often found in written Japanese, news reports, or formal speeches to describe actions occurring simultaneously.

1. Simultaneous Actions (Formal "While")

Like `ながら`, it attaches to the `Verb Stem`. It describes two actions performed by the same subject at the same time.

`[Verb Stem] + つつ、[Main Action]`

  • 将来しょうらいの事ことを`考かんがえつつ`、進路しんろを決きめる。
    (Deciding one's path `while thinking` about the future.)
  • 環境かんきょうに配慮はいりょ`しつつ`、開発かいはつを進すすめる。
    (Proceeding with development `while taking` the environment into consideration.)

2. "Although" (～つつも)

When used with `も`, or sometimes just `つつ`, it can express a contradiction similar to "although" or "even though." It often describes a conflict between one's thoughts and actions.

  • 体に良よくないと`知しりつつ（も）`、タバコがやめられない。
    (`Even though I know` it's bad for my body, I can't quit smoking.)
  • 早はやく返事へんじをしようと`思おもいつつ`、まだ書かいていない。
    (`Although I've been thinking` I should reply quickly, I haven't written it yet.)

3. Ongoing Change (～つつある)

When followed by `ある`, it indicates that a certain change is currently in progress. This is a common N3/N2 structure.

  • 景気けいきは回復かいふく`しつつある`。
    (The economy is `in the process of` recovering.)
  • 温暖化おんだんかが進すすみ`つつある`。
    (Global warming is `steadily progressing`.)
