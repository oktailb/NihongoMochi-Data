Doing in Advance: ～ておく

The auxiliary verb ～ておく is used when you perform an action in advance to prepare for the future, or to keep a certain state as it is.

1. Preparation for the Future

Doing something now so that you don't have to worry about it later.

  • 旅行りょこうの前まえに、ホテルを予約よやくしておきます。
    (I will book a hotel in advance before the trip.)
  • 明日あしたテストがあるから、勉強べんきょうしておきます。
    (Since there is a test tomorrow, I will study in advance.)

2. Leaving a State as it Is

Sometimes it means doing something to maintain a situation.

  • 窓まどを開あけておいてください。
    (Please leave the window open [keep it that way].)

3. Casual Form: ～とく

In casual speech, ～ておく often contracts to ～とく.

  • ～ておく → ～とく (e.g., 書かいとく)
  • ～でおく → ～どく (e.g., 飲のんどく)

Ex: ビール、買かいとくね！ (I'll buy the beer in advance! [Casual])
