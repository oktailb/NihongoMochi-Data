In Addition To: ～に加えて (にくわえて)

Sometimes one good (or bad) thing isn't enough! `～に加えて` (written as `～に加えて`) is used to say "in addition to" or "as well as." It's more formal and emphasis-heavy than just using the particle `も` or `そして`.

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + に加えて

2. Additive Examples

  • 風かぜ`に加えて`、雨あめもひどくなってきた。
    (In addition to the wind, the rain has also become severe.)
  • 英語えいご`に加えて`、彼かれは中国語ちゅうごくごも話はなせる。
    (In addition to English, he can also speak Chinese.)
  • このアパートは、家賃やちんの安やすさ`に加えて`、場所ばしょもいい。
    (Besides the low rent, this apartment is also in a great location.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of the verb `くわえる` (to add). This expression stacks facts on top of each other. It's perfect for listing multiple reasons, advantages, or disadvantages in a clear, organized way.
