Speaking of...: ～というと / ～といえば

In conversation, one topic often leads to another! `～というと` is used when you hear a word or see something, and it immediately reminds you of something else. It's like saying "When you say X, it makes me think of Y."

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + というと / といえば / といったら

2. Associative Examples

  • 日本にほんの料理りょうり`というと`、やはりお寿司すしが一番いちばんに思おもい浮うかぶ。
    (When you say Japanese food, sushi is naturally the first thing that comes to mind.)
  • 京都きょうと`といえば`、寺てらや神社じんじゃが多おおいですね。
    (Speaking of Kyoto, there are many temples and shrines, aren't there?)
  • 「来週らいしゅうの旅行りょこう」`というと`、どこへ行いくんですか？
    (Talking about "next week's trip," where are you going?)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a great conversational tool! It helps you transition smoothly between topics by picking up on a "keyword" from the previous sentence. It shows you are listening and making connections. `といえば` is the most common version in casual chat.
