Reciprocal Action: ～あう

The suffix `～あう` (written as `～合う`) is attached to a verb to express that two or more people do something together or to each other.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `あう` to the `Verb Stem` (the part before `~ます`).

`[Verb Stem] + あう`

  • 私わたしたちはよく話はなし`あいます`。
    (We often talk to each other / discuss.)
  • みんなで助たすけ`あいましょう`。
    (Let's all help each other.)
  • かれらは愛あいし`あっています`。
    (They love each other.)

2. Common Combinations

Verb Meaning
話はなしあう To discuss / Talk to each other
助たすけあう To help each other
見みつめあう To gaze at each other
知しりあう To get to know each other

3. Conjugation

`あう` is a Group 1 (Godan) verb.

  • `Polite:` ～あいます
  • `Past:` ～あった
  • `Negative:` ～あわない
