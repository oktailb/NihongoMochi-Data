Because of (Blame): ～せいで

The expression `～せいで` is the opposite of `おかげ で`. It is used to blame something or someone for a `negative result`. It translates to "because of...", "due to...", or "it's [someone's] fault that..."

1. Sentence Structure

The connections are identical to `おかげ　で`.

  • 雨あめの`せいで`、試合しあいが中止ちゅうしになりました。
    (Because of the rain [it's the rain's fault], the match was cancelled.)
  • 昨日きのう、お酒さけを飲のみすぎた`せいで`、頭あたまが痛いたいです。
    (Because I drank too much yesterday, my head hurts.)

2. Connection Table

Type Connection
Verbs / い-Adj Plain Form + せいで
な-Adj Adj + `な` + せいで
Nouns Noun + `の` + せいで

3. "It's your fault!" (せいにする)

You can also use the phrase `せいにする` to mean "to put the blame on someone."

  • かれはいつも他人たにんの`せいにします`。
    (He always puts the blame on others.)

4. Summary: おかげで vs せいで

  • `おかげで:` Happy result (Gratitude).
  • `せいで:` Bad result (Blame).
