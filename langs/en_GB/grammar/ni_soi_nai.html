Without a Doubt: ～に相違ない (にそういない)

When you are absolutely certain about something, especially in a formal or logical context, we use ～に相違ない (written as ～に相違ない). It means "there is no doubt that" or "it must be." It is a powerful way to express conviction.

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb/Adj/Noun Plain Form] + に相違ない

2. Convincing Examples

  • 犯人はんにんはあの男おとこに相違ない。
    (The criminal is, without a doubt, that man.)
  • この計画けいかくは成功せいこうするに相違ない。
    (This plan will surely succeed / There is no doubt about its success.)
  • 彼かれが合格ごうかくしたのは、努力どりょくの結果けっかに相違ない。
    (His passing was, without a doubt, the result of his effort.)

3. Sensei's Note

The word soui means "difference" or "discrepancy." So this expression literally says "there is no discrepancy between my thought and the truth." It's very firm and often used in official reports or formal debates. It's the "detective's conclusion" of grammar!
