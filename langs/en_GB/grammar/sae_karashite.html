Even the Smallest Detail: ～さえ / ～からして

When you want to show that a situation is so intense that even the most basic or unexpected example is true, we use ～さえ or ～からして. It's like saying "Even X is true, so obviously the rest is too!"

1. Sentence Structure

  • Noun + さえ
  • Noun + からして

2. Extreme Examples

  • 子供こどもさえ知っている。
    (Even a child knows that.)
  • 彼は自分の名前さえ書けない。
    (He can't even write his own name.)
  • この料理りょうりは、においからしておいしそうだ。
    (Judging from even the smell, this dish looks delicious.)

3. Sensei's Note

～さえ is used for general "even." ～からして is specifically for saying "judging even from X (which is a minor detail), the whole thing must be Y." For example: "Judging even from his attitude (taido kara shite), he's a professional." It highlights a small, tell-tale sign!
