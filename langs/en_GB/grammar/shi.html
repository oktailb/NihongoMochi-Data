Listing Reasons: ～し

The conjunction `～し` is used to list several reasons for a conclusion or a situation. It often implies that there are other reasons as well, not just the ones mentioned.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `し` to the `Plain Form` of sentences.

  • この店みせは安やすい`し`、おいしい`し`、いつも来きています。
    (This shop is cheap and delicious, so I always come here.)
  • 雨あめも降ふっている`し`、荷物にもつも多おおい`し`、タクシーで帰かえりましょう。
    (It's raining and I have a lot of bags, so let's go home by taxi.)

2. Connection Table

Type Connection
Verbs / い-Adj Plain Form + し
な-Adj / Nouns Word + `だ` + し

3. Usage Note

Unlike `～から` (which focuses on one strong reason), `～し` lists multiple points, making the overall argument more convincing and balanced.
