In Exchange or Instead: ～代わりに

This pattern is perfect for life's trade-offs! ～代わりに (written as ～代わりに) is used for two main things: doing one thing instead of another, or doing something in exchange for something else.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (Plain Form) + 代わりに
  • Noun + の代わりに

2. Trade-off Examples

  • 日曜日にちようびに働はたらいた代わりに、月曜日げつようびに休やすみます。
    (In exchange for working on Sunday, I will take Monday off.)
  • コーヒーの代わりに、お茶ちゃを飲のみました。
    (I drank tea instead of coffee.)
  • 日本語にほんごを教おしえてもらう代わりに、英語えいごを教おしえています。
    (In exchange for being taught Japanese, I am teaching English.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of it as a "substitution" or a "balance." When you use it with a verb, it often implies a "compensation." For example: この部屋は狭い代わりに、家賃が安い (This room is small, but on the flip side, the rent is cheap). It balances the scale!
