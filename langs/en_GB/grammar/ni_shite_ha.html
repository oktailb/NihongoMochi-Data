Contrary to expectation: ～にしては

The expression ～にしては is used when something does not match the standard or expectation you would typically associate with a person, thing, or situation. It translates to "for a...", "considering that...", or "it's surprising that... given that..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach にしては directly to a Noun or the Plain Form of a verb.

[Noun / Verb Plain] + にしては

  • かれは日本人にほんじんにしては、背せが高たかい。
    (For a Japanese person, he is tall [He is taller than the average expectation].)
  • 一年間いちねんかん勉強べんきょうしたにしては、あまり話はなせません。
    (Considering I studied for a year, I can't speak much.)
  • この鞄かばんは、値段ねだんにしてはしっかりしている。
    (Considering the price, this bag is well-made.)

2. Key Nuance: Surprise

Unlike ～わりに, which compares a result to a standard, ～にしては emphasizes the speaker's feeling of surprise that the reality is different from the specific fact mentioned. It is often used to praise or criticize someone based on their category.

3. Distinction from ～わりに

  • ～わりに: Focuses on the ratio/proportion (Good result compared to effort).
  • ～にしては: Focuses on the fact itself (Unexpected result given the specific fact).
