Moral Obligation: ～べき

The expression ～べき is used to say that someone "should" or "ought to" do something. It implies a moral or social obligation, or that it is the natural and logical thing to do.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach べき to the Dictionary Form of a verb.

  • 約束やくそくは守まもるべきです。
    (Promises should be kept.)
  • もっと勉強べんきょうするべきだと思おもいます。
    (I think [you] should study more.)

⚠️ Irregular: For the verb する, you can use either するべき or すべき.

2. Negative: ～べきではない

To say that someone "should not" do something.

  • そんなことを言いうべきではありません。
    (You should not say such things.)

3. Nuance vs ～なければならない

  • ～なければならない: A strict obligation (must), often due to rules or external pressure.
  • ～べき: A moral "should" based on what is right or logical. It sounds a bit like giving a lecture.
