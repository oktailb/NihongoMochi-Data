On the other hand: ～反面

The expression `～反面` (はんめん) is used to describe two opposite qualities or aspects of the same thing. It translates to "on the other hand," "while at the same time," or "conversely."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `反面` to the `Plain Form` of verbs and adjectives. For nouns, use `である` or `の`.

`[Aspect A] 反面、 [Aspect B]`

  • この仕事しごとは給料きゅうりょうがいい`反面`、とても忙いそがしい。
    (While this job has good pay, on the other hand, it's very busy.)
  • インターネットは便利べんりな`反面`、危険きけんなこともあります。
    (While the internet is convenient, on the other hand, there are also dangers.)
  • かれは自信家じしんかである`反面`、実じつはとても繊細せんさいな性格せいかくだ。
    (While he is confident, conversely, he actually has a very delicate personality.)

2. Key Nuance: Coexisting Opposites

It emphasizes that one thing has both a "front" and a "back" side. It is used to present a balanced view or to highlight a contradiction within a single subject.
