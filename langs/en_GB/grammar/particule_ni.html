The Particle に (Destination, Time, and Target)

The particle に is one of the most versatile in Japanese. Its primary roles are to indicate destination, specific time, and the indirect object (the target) of an action.

1. Destination (Movement)

When used with verbs of movement like 行いく (to go) or 来くる (to come), に marks the place you are heading to.

  • 日本にほんに行いきます。
    (I am going to Japan.)

2. Specific Time

に is used for specific points in time (days, hours, months). It is not used for relative time like "today" or "tomorrow".

  • 七時しちじに起おきます。
    (I wake up at 7 o'clock.)
  • 月曜日げつようびにテストがあります。
    (There is a test on Monday.)

3. The Target / Indirect Object

It marks the person who receives an action (to someone).

  • 友達ともだちに会あいます。
    (I will meet [with] a friend.)
  • 先生せんせいに聞ききます。
    (I will ask [to] the teacher.)

4. Presence / Existence

When saying where something is (with ある or いる), に marks the location.

  • 机つくえの上うえに本ほんがあります。
    (There is a book on the desk.)
