Completion and Regret: ～てしまう

The auxiliary verb ～てしまう is used to express that an action has been completely finished, or that something happened which the speaker regrets.

1. Complete Action

It emphasizes that something is finished 100%, often with a sense of "finally" or "completely."

  • この本ほんを全部ぜんぶ読よんでしまいました。
    (I have finished reading this entire book.)
  • 宿題をやってしまいましょう。
    (Let's get the homework finished once and for all.)

2. Regret or Accident

It is very commonly used when something happens by mistake, or when you feel bad about an action.

  • 道みちを間違まちがえてしまいました。
    (I [unfortunately] made a mistake with the directions.)
  • 財布さいふを忘わすれてしまいました。
    (I accidentally forgot my wallet.)

3. Casual Forms: ～ちゃう and ～じゃう

In daily casual speech, ～てしまう is almost always shortened:

  • ～てしまう → ～ちゃう (e.g., 行いっちゃう)
  • ～でしまう → ～じゃう (e.g., 飲のんじゃう)

Ex: 宿題しゅくだい、わすれちゃった！ (I forgot my homework! [Casual])
