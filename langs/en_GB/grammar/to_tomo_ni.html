In Parallel: ～と共に (とともに)

Some things in life happen in perfect harmony or at the same time. `～と共に` means "together with" (people) or "along with" (changes). It's a formal and elegant way to describe simultaneous progression.

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Noun` + と共に
  • `Verb (Dictionary Form)` + と共に

2. Harmonious Examples

  • 家族かぞく`と共に`楽たのしい時間じかんを過すごす。
    (To spend a pleasant time together with one's family.)
  • 時代じだいの変化へんか`と共に`、言葉ことばも変かわっていく。
    (Along with the changing times, language also changes.)
  • 太陽たいようが昇のぼる`と共に`、鳥とりが鳴なき始はじめた。
    (Together with the rising of the sun, the birds began to sing.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is much more sophisticated than just using `と` (with) or `いっしょ　に`. It captures the feeling of two things being intertwined. If you want to describe a person who has both talent and a good heart at the same time, you could say `才能と共に誠実さも持っている`. It's the language of balance!
