The Contextual Conditional: ～なら (ば)

The `～なら` conditional (sometimes formal `～ならば`) is used to give advice, make a suggestion, or comment based on a `context` or something the other person just said.

1. Sentence Structure

Simply attach `なら` to the `Plain Form` of a verb or adjective, or directly to a `Noun` (without `だ`).

  • 「来週らいしゅう、京都きょうとへ行いきます。」
    「京都きょうとへ`行くなら`、お寺てらを見みてください。」
    ("I'm going to Kyoto next week." "`If` you are going to Kyoto, please see the temples.")
  • 「おなかがすきました。」
    「おなかがすいた`なら`、これを食たべてください。」
    ("I'm hungry." "`If` you're hungry, please eat this.")

2. Key Usage: "As for..." or "If it's the case that..."

It is often used when the speaker is picking up a topic from the conversation.

  • 「あした、暇ひまですか。」
    「あした`なら`、暇ひまですよ。」
    ("Are you free tomorrow?" "`If` it's tomorrow [you're asking about], I'm free.")

3. Comparison: ～なら vs ～たら

While `～たら` requires action A to be finished `before` action B, `～なら` can be used for things that will happen in the future or are currently true.

  • `～たら:` After you arrive, call me.
  • `～なら:` If you are going to go (in the future), buy a ticket now.
