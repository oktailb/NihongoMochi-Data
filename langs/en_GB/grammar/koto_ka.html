How Wonderful!: ～ことか

When your emotions are overflowing and words like "very" or "very much" just aren't enough, we use ～ことか. It is an exclamation of degree, used to emphasize how intense a feeling or situation is. It's like saying "How...!" or "What a...!"

1. Sentence Structure

This is often used with question words like どんなに (how much), どれほど (to what extent), or 何回 (how many times).

  • Verb/Adj (Plain Form) + ことか

2. Emotional Examples

  • 合格ごうかくしたと知しって、どんなにうれしかったことか！
    (You can't imagine how happy I was to hear that I passed!)
  • この日ひをどれほど待まっていたことか。
    (How long I have waited for this day!)
  • これまで何度なんどやめようと思おもったことか。
    (How many times I've thought about quitting until now...)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a "soulful" expression. It's used to show that the speaker is deeply moved. It's more common in writing or formal speeches than in casual conversation. If you want to express deep regret, deep joy, or deep struggle, ～ことか is the brush you should use!
