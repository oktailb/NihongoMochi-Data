Obligation: ～なければなりません

To express "must" or "have to" in Japanese, we use a structure that literally translates to: "if [you] don't do it, it won't do."

1. Sentence Structure

Start with the `Nai-form`, remove the final `い`, and add `ければなりません`.

`[Verb ない形ないけい] (minus い) + ければなりません`

  • 毎日まいにち勉強べんきょう`しなければなりません`。
    (I must study every day.)
  • 薬くすりを飲の`まなければなりません`。
    (I have to take medicine.)
  • 家うちに帰かえ`らなければなりません`。
    (I must go home.)

2. Politeness Levels

You can change the ending to adjust the politeness or context:

  • `Polite:` ～なければなりません (Standard)
  • `Very Polite:` ～なければいけません (Common in daily life)
  • `Casual:` ～なきゃ / ～なくちゃ (Used with friends)

3. "Don't have to" (～なくてもいい)

The opposite ("don't have to") is formed by adding `なくてもいい` to the Nai-form stem.

  • 明日あしたは来こ`なくてもいい`です。
    (You don't have to come tomorrow.)
