Just in Case / For Now: 一応 (いちおう)

This is a magical word that every Japanese learner should know! 一応 (いちおう) is used when you do something as a precaution, or when a task is "mostly" done but not yet perfect. It covers "just in case," "for the time being," or "tentatively."

1. Sentence Structure

一応 + Verb / Adjective

2. Practical Examples

  • 一応いちおう、傘かさを持って行いきます。
    (I'll take an umbrella, just in case.)
  • 仕事しごとは一応いちおう終おわりました。
    (The work is finished for now / tentatively finished.)
  • 一応いちおう確認かくにんさせてください。
    (Please let me check, just to be sure.)

3. Sensei's Note

Japanese people love 一応 because it adds a layer of humility and caution. It shows you've made an effort but acknowledge it might not be 100% complete, or that you're prepared for the unexpected. It's the ultimate "safety" word!
