Permission: ～てもいいです

The expression `～てもいいです` is used to give or ask for permission. It translates to "you may...", "it's okay to...", or "is it alright if...?"

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `もいいです` to the `Te-form` of a verb, an adjective, or a noun.

`[Verb て形てけい] + もいいです`

  • テレビを`見みてもいいです`よ。
    (You may watch TV.)
  • ここを`使つかってもいいですか`。
    (Is it alright if I use this place?)

2. With Adjectives and Nouns

  • `い-Adj:` 安やすくてもいいです (It's okay if it's cheap).
  • `な-Adj / Noun:` 暇ひまでもいいです (It's okay if you're free).

3. Casual Speech

In casual conversation, the `～です` is dropped, and you just use `～てもいい` or even just the `Te-form` with a questioning intonation.

  • 食たべてもいい？ (Can I eat this?)

4. Note on Nuance

Asking "～てもいいですか" is very polite and respectful. It shows that you are seeking the other person's approval before acting.
