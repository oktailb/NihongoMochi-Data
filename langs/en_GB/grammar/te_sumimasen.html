Apologizing with Reason: ～てすみません

A true apology in Japanese often includes the reason for the mistake! ～てすみません is the standard way to say "I'm sorry for doing X." It connects your action directly to your regret.

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb Te-form] + すみません / ごめんなさい

2. Sincere Examples

  • 遅おくれてすみません。
    (I'm sorry for being late.)
  • 返事へんじが遅おそくなってすみません。
    (I'm sorry for the late reply.)
  • 昨日きのうは行いけなくてごめんなさい。
    (I'm sorry I couldn't go yesterday.)

3. Sensei's Note

This pattern is essential for smooth social life in Japan! By stating the reason (being late, forgetting, etc.) in the te-form, you show that you acknowledge exactly what you did wrong. For a more formal apology in business, you can use ～て申し訳ございません (~te moushiwake gozaimasen).
