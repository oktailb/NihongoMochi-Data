"Of all things...": ～に限って

The expression ～に限かぎって has two main meanings: it can express ironic bad luck ("of all days..."), or it can state a firm belief that a specific person would never do something.

1. Ironic Bad Luck (Bad Timing)

Use this when something bad happens specifically at the most inconvenient moment. It translates to "Of all [times/days/people]..."

  • 傘かさを忘わすれたに限かぎって、雨あめが降ふる。
    (Of all times to forget my umbrella, it [just has to] rain.)
  • 急いそいでいるに限かぎって、バスがなかなか来こない。
    (Of all times when I'm in a hurry, the bus just doesn't come.)

2. Firm Belief (Exceptional Trust)

When used with a person you know well, it expresses your certainty that they are the exception to a negative situation.

  • あの子こに限かぎって、そんなひどいことをするはずがない。
    (That child specifically would never do such a terrible thing.)

3. Connection

Attach に限って directly to Nouns or the Plain Form of verbs (often in the Ta-form for bad timing).

[Noun / Verb Plain] + に限って
