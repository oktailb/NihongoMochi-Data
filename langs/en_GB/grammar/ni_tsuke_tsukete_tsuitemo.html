Every time / Regardless: ～につけ

The expression `～につけ` is used to describe something that happens automatically every time a certain event occurs, or to show that a statement holds true regardless of the situation (when used in pairs).

1. "Every time something happens": ～につけ

It expresses that a specific feeling or thought naturally arises whenever you see or hear something.

  • この写真しゃしんを見みる`につけ`、子供こどもの頃ころを思おもい出だす。
    (Every time I look at this photo, I remember my childhood.)
  • ニュースを聞きく`につけ`、心こころが痛いたみます。
    (Every time I hear the news, my heart aches.)

2. "Regardless / Whether... or...": ～につけ ～につけ

When used in pairs, it shows that something is true in either case. It often involves opposite words like "good/bad."

  • 良よき`につけ`悪あき`につけ`、結果けっかを報告ほうこくしてください。
    (Whether the result is good or bad, please report it.)
  • 雨あめの日`につけ`風かぜの日`につけ`、かれは毎日まいにち走はしる。
    (Whether it is a rainy day or a windy day, he runs every day.)
