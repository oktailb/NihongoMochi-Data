The Particle と (And / With)

The particle `と` has two primary functions: connecting nouns in a complete list and indicating the person you perform an action with.

1. Connecting Nouns (Exhaustive "And")

Use `と` between nouns to list everything. It implies that the list is complete.

  • 机つくえの上うえに本ほん`と`ペンがあります。
    (There is a book `and` a pen on the desk [and nothing else].)
  • パン`と`卵たまごを食たべました。
    (I ate bread `and` an egg.)

2. Doing together (With)

Use `と` after a person to show you are doing an action with them.

  • 友達ともだち`と`映画えいがを見みに行きました。
    (I went to see a movie `with` a friend.)
  • 家族かぞく`と`住すんでいます。
    (I live `with` my family.)

3. Quote Marker

It is also used to mark a direct or indirect quote before verbs like 言いう (to say) or 思おもう (to think).

  • 田中さんは「暑あつい」`と`言いいました。
    (Mr. Tanaka said "it is hot".)
