From... to...: ～から～にかけて

The expression `～から～にかけて` is used to describe a range in time or space. Unlike `～から～まで`, it implies a fuzzy or continuous range rather than precise endpoints.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `から` to the starting point and `にかけて` to the ending point (both are nouns).

`[Noun A] から [Noun B] にかけて`

  • 昨日きのうの夜よる`から`今朝けさ`にかけて`、雨あめが降ふりました。
    (It rained from last night through this morning.)
  • 関東地方かんとうちほう`から`東北地方とうほくちほう`にかけて`、地震じしんがありました。
    (An earthquake occurred from the Kanto region to the Tohoku region.)

2. Difference from ～から～まで

  • `～から～まで:` Focuses on the precise start and end points (e.g., from 9:00 to 5:00).
  • `～から～にかけて:` Describes a general area or duration where the action or state occurred continuously or repeatedly.

3. Key Usage

It is often used when talking about weather, natural phenomena, or general time periods (like seasons).
