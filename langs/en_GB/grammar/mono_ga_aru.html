A Certain Quality: ～ものがある

Sometimes you can't quite put your finger on it, but you feel a strong quality in something. `～ものがある` is used to express that something has a certain characteristic or evokes a specific feeling. It means "there is something... about it."

1. Sentence Structure

`Verb/Adj (Plain Form)` + ものがある

2. Evocative Examples

  • 彼かれの歌声うたごえには心こころを打うつ`ものがある`。
    (There is something deeply moving about his singing voice.)
  • この不況ふきょうで、経営けいえいは厳きびしい`ものがある`。
    (With this recession, the management situation is truly severe.)
  • 彼かれの話はなしには、どこか納得なっとくできない`ものがある`。
    (There is something about his story that I just can't quite accept.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very nuanced expression! It shows that the speaker is making a subjective but strong observation. It's often used for things that are difficult to quantify but are definitely felt. It adds a layer of depth to your descriptions.
