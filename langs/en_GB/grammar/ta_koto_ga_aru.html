Past Experience: ～たことがある

The expression `～たことがある` is used to say that you have had a specific experience at some point in your life. It translates to "I have done [something] before."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `ことがある` to the `Past Form (Ta-form)` of a verb.

`[Verb た形たけい] + ことがある`

  • 日本にほんに`行いったことがあります`。
    (I have been to Japan before.)
  • 納豆なっとうを`食たべたことがありますか`。
    (Have you ever eaten Natto?)
  • 一度いちどもスキーを`したことがありません`。
    (I have never been skiing even once.)

2. Key Difference: ～たことがある vs ～た

  • `～た (Past Tense):` Simply means the action happened in the past (e.g., "I went yesterday").
  • `～たことがある:` Emphasizes the `experience` itself, regardless of when it happened.

3. Adverbs of Frequency

It is often used with adverbs like `一度いちど` (once), `何回なんかいも` (many times), or `一度いちども...ない` (never).
