As long as / Within limits: ～かぎり

The expression `～かぎり` (written as `～限り`) is used to set a condition or a limit. It translates to "as long as...", "while a state continues...", or "to the extent of..."

1. As long as (Condition)

Use this to express that a certain result will continue as long as the current situation remains unchanged.

  • 体からだが丈夫じょうぶな`かぎり`、働はたらきつづけたい。
    (`As long as` my body is healthy, I want to keep working.)
  • この契約けいやくを守まもる`かぎり`、問題もんだいはありません。
    (`As long as` you follow this contract, there are no problems.)

2. Within the limits of (Extent)

Use this to emphasize that you are doing everything possible within your power or knowledge.

  • 力ちからの`かぎり`、頑張がんばります。
    (I will do my best `to the extent of` my power.)
  • 私の知しっている`かぎり`では、かれは犯人はんにんではありません。
    (`As far as` I know, he is not the criminal.)
