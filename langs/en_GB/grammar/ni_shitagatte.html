Following / Along with: ～にしたがって

The expression `～にしたがって` (にしたがって) has two main uses: describing a change that happens in parallel with another change, or following a set of rules, instructions, or orders.

1. Parallel Change (As...)

Similar to `~に　つれて`, it describes a second change that happens along with the first.

  • 北きたへ行いく`にしたがって`、雪ゆきが多おおくなります。
    (`As` we go north, the snow becomes more frequent.)
  • 予定よていが変かわる`にしたがって`、費用ひようも変かわります。
    (`Along with` the changes in the schedule, the costs also change.)

2. Following Rules or Orders

It translates to "according to...", "following...", or "obeying...".

  • 指示しじ`にしたがって`、動うごいてください。
    (Please move `according to` the instructions.)
  • 法律ほうりつ`にしたがって`、正ただしく処理しょりします。
    (We will process it correctly `in accordance with` the law.)

3. Connection

Attach `にしたがって` to the `Dictionary Form` of a verb or directly to a `Noun`.

4. Summary of Change Structures

Grammar Nuance
`～につれて` Natural, one-directional progression.
`～にしたがって` Formal parallel change or following a guideline.
