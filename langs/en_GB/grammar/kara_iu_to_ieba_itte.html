From a Certain Standpoint: ～からいうと / ～からいえば

How we see things depends on where we stand! ～からいうと (and its variations) is used to express an opinion or judgment from a specific perspective or standpoint. It means "from the viewpoint of..." or "judging from..."

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + からいうと / からいえば / からいって

2. Perspectival Examples

  • 能力のうりょくからいうと、彼かれが一番いちばんふさわしい。
    (From the standpoint of ability, he is the most suitable [candidate].)
  • 今いまの実力じつりょくからいえば、合格ごうかくは難むずかしいだろう。
    (Judging from his current level of ability, passing will likely be difficult.)
  • 私の経験けいけんからいって、それはあまりおすすめしません。
    (Speaking from my experience, I wouldn't really recommend that.)

3. Sensei's Note

The variations are similar: ～からいうと focuses on the viewpoint itself, ～からいえば is often used for a conclusion or conditional judgment, and ～からいって is frequently used when giving a reason for an opinion. All of them help you ground your statement in a specific context!
