Starting with: ～をはじめ

The expression `～をはじめ` (or `～をはじめとする`) is used to introduce a representative example from a larger group. It translates to "starting with...", "beginning with...", or "not only [A] but also [others]."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `をはじめ` directly to a `Noun`. To modify a following noun, use `をはじめとする`.

`[Noun] + をはじめ`

  • 日本にほんには、富士山ふじさん`をはじめ`、たくさんの有名ゆうめいな山やまがあります。
    (In Japan, there are many famous mountains, `starting with` Mt. Fuji.)
  • この学校がっこうには、校長先生こうちょうせんせい`をはじめとする`たくさんの先生せんせいがいます。
    (In this school, there are many teachers, `beginning with` the principal.)

2. Key Usage: Highlighting the Most Prominent

Use `～をはじめ` when you want to pick the most important or obvious example to represent a whole group.
