In spite of / Regardless: ～にもかかわらず

The expression `～にもかかわらず` is used to say that an action or situation occurs despite a certain fact or condition. It translates to "in spite of...", "despite...", or "nevertheless." It is a formal and emphatic expression.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `にもかかわらず` to the `Plain Form` of verbs and adjectives. For nouns and na-adjectives, it attaches directly (no `だ` needed).

`[Fact] + にもかかわらず`

  • 雨あめ`にもかかわらず`、たくさんの人ひとが集あつまりました。
    (Despite the rain, many people gathered.)
  • 大変たいへんな`にもかかわらず`、かれはいつも笑顔えがおで働はたらいている。
    (Despite the difficulty, he is always working with a smile.)
  • 忙いそがしい`にもかかわらず`、わざわざ来きてくれてありがとうございます。
    (In spite of being busy, thank you for coming all this way.)

2. Key Nuance: Strong Contrast

It highlights a surprising outcome that ignores the obstacle or condition mentioned in the first part. It is more formal and stronger than `~のに` or `~て　も`.

3. Distinction from ～にかかわらず

  • `～に「も」かかわらず:` Means "despite the fact" (Contrastive).
  • `～にかかわらず:` Means "regardless of" or "it doesn't matter whether" (Neutral inclusion).
