Designed For: ～向け (むけ)

When something is created specifically with a certain person or group in mind, we use `～向け`. It indicates the target audience or the intended user of a product or service. It means "for," "aimed at," or "intended for."

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + 向け (だ / に / の + Noun)

2. Targeted Examples

  • この本ほんは子供こども`向け`に書かかれています。
    (This book is written for children.)
  • これは外国人がいこくじん`向け`の料理りょうりだ。
    (This is a dish intended for foreigners.)
  • このマンションは一人暮ひとりぐらし`向け`だ。
    (This apartment is aimed at people living alone.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of `～向け` as an arrow pointing at a target. It's about the `intention` of the creator. Don't confuse it with `～向き` (which we will see next!), which is more about something being "suitable" or "appropriate" by its nature!
