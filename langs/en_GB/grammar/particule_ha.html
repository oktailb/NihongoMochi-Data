The Particle は (Topic Marker)

The particle は (pronounced "wa") is the most fundamental particle in Japanese. It identifies the topic of your sentence—what you are talking about.

1. Function

Think of は as meaning "As for..." or "Speaking of...". Once a topic is established with は, you don't need to repeat it in the following sentences.

2. Key Examples

  • 私わたしは学生がくせいです。
    (As for me, I am a student.)
  • 今日きょうは暑あついです。
    (Speaking of today, it is hot.)
  • 田中たなかさんは親切しんせつです。
    (As for Mr. Tanaka, he is kind.)

3. Important Note

Even though it is written with the hiragana ha (は), it is always pronounced wa when used as a particle.
