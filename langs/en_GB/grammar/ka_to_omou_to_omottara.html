Instant Changes: ～かと思ったら / ～かと思うと

Life can change in a heartbeat! ～かと思ったら is used to describe an action or a change that happens almost immediately after another, often in a way that is surprising or unexpected. It means "just when I thought..." or "no sooner had X happened than Y."

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb Past Form] + かと思ったら / かと思うと

2. Surprising Examples

  • 空そらが暗くらくなったかと思ったら、いきなり雨あめが降ふり出した。
    (Just when I thought the sky had turned dark, it suddenly started to rain.)
  • 赤ちゃんあかちゃんは泣ないたかと思うと、もう笑わらっている。
    (No sooner had the baby cried than he was already laughing.)
  • 彼かれは部屋へやに来きたかと思ったら、すぐに出て行ってしまった。
    (Just when I thought he had arrived in the room, he left immediately.)

3. Sensei's Note

This expression captures that feeling of "whiplash" when things move too fast! It highlights the speaker's surprise at the speed of the sequence. While ~ka nai ka no uchi ni is about the physical speed, ～かと思ったら focuses more on the speaker's perception and expectation being overturned. Use it for those "double-take" moments!
