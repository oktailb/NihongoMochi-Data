Certainty: ～に違ちがいない

The expression `～に違ちがいない` is used to express strong certainty or conviction. It translates to "must be," "without a doubt," or "I am sure that..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `に違ちがいない` directly to the `Plain Form` of verbs, adjectives, and nouns (no `だ` needed for nouns or na-adjectives).

  • 犯人はんにんはあの男おとこ`に違ちがいない`。
    (The criminal must be that man.)
  • 彼かれはもう着ついている`に違ちgいない`。
    (He must have arrived already.)

2. Key Examples

  • これを食たべたら、おいしい`に違ちがいない`。
    (I'm sure this will be delicious if you eat it.)
  • 合格ごうかくするのは大変たいへん`に違ちがいない`。
    (It must be very difficult to pass the exam.)

3. Nuance: Subjective Conviction

While `～はず` is based on logical expectation (objective), `～に違いない` often reflects the speaker's own strong intuition or firm belief (subjective). It sounds more confident and definitive.

4. Politeness

In polite speech, you can use `～に違ちがいありません`, although `～に違いないです` is also heard in casual-polite settings.
