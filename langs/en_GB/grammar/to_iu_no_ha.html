Defining and Clarifying: ～というのは

When you want to explain the meaning of a word, or clarify the reason for a situation, ～というのは is your primary tool. It's like saying "As for the thing called X..." or "What 'X' means is..."

1. Sentence Structure

Noun / Phrase + というのは

2. Definitional Examples

  • 「一期一会いちごいちえ」というのは、どういう意味ですか？
    (What does "Ichi-go Ichi-e" mean?)
  • 彼かれが来こなかったというのは、本当ほんとうですか？
    (Is it true that he didn't come?)
  • 禁煙きんえんというのは、タバコを吸すわないことです。
    (What "Kin-en" means is to not smoke cigarettes.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is the starting point for almost every explanation in Japanese! It sets the topic firmly on the table. You'll often see it followed by ～のことだ (is about...) or ～の意味だ (means...). Use it to clear up any confusion and build a bridge of understanding!
