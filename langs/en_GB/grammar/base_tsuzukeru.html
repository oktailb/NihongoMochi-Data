To Continue: ～つづける

The suffix `～つづける` (written as `～続ける`) is used to express that an action or a state is being continued or repeated over a period of time.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `つづける` to the `Verb Stem` (the part before `~ます`).

`[Verb Stem] + つづける`

  • 雨あめが三日間みっかかん`降ふりつづけています`。
    (It has been continuing to rain for three days.)
  • あきらめないで、`頑張がんばりつづけてください`。
    (Please don't give up and keep on doing your best.)
  • かれは一時間いちじかん走はし`りつづけました`。
    (He continued running for an hour.)

2. Nuance: Repetition or Duration

It can describe a single continuous action (like running) or an action that is repeated regularly over time (like studying every day).

3. Conjugation

`つづける` is a Group 2 (Ichidan) verb.

  • `Polite:` ～つづけます
  • `Past:` ～つづけました
  • `Negative:` ～つづけない
