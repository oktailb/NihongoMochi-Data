Without Care or Concern: ～もかまわず

Sometimes someone acts without worrying about what others think or what the situation is. ～もかまわず (written as ～も構わず) means "without caring about" or "regardless of." It describes an action done with total disregard for a specific factor.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + もかまわず

2. Bold Examples

  • 人目ひとめもかまわず、道みちで泣なき出した。
    (Without caring about people watching, they started crying in the street.)
  • 雨あめにぬれるのもかまわず、走はしり続つづけた。
    (Regardless of getting soaked in the rain, they kept on running.)
  • 深夜しんやもかまわず、大声おおごえで歌うたっている。
    (Regardless of it being late at night, they are singing at the top of their lungs.)

3. Sensei's Note

This expression highlights a certain lack of common sense or a very strong emotion that makes someone ignore their surroundings. It's often used when an action is surprising or perhaps even a bit rude, because the person is ignoring a factor that they should probably consider.
