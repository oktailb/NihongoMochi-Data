Incomplete Action: ～かける

The suffix `～かける` is used to describe an action that has been started but not yet finished, or an action that was just about to happen. It translates to "half-done...", "in the middle of...", or "on the verge of..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `かける` to the `Verb Stem` (the part before `~ます`).

`[Verb Stem] + かける`

  • テーブルの上うえに、飲のみ`かけ`のコーヒーがあります。
    (There is a `half-drunk` coffee on the table.)
  • かれは何かを言いい`かけて`、やめました。
    (He `started to say` something, then stopped.)
  • あきらめ`かけた`とき、いいアイデアが浮うかびました。
    (Just as I was `on the verge of` giving up, a good idea came to me.)

2. Key Usage: "Half-done" Nouns

When used to modify a noun, it often means the state of being incomplete.

  • 読よみかけの本 (A half-read book)
  • 食たべかけのリンゴ (A half-eaten apple)

3. Conjugation

`かける` is a Group 2 (Ichidan) verb.
