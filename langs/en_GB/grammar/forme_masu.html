The Masu-form (ます形ますけい) - Polite Japanese

The Masu-form is the standard polite form of verbs. It is used when speaking to teachers, strangers, or in professional settings. Using this form ensures you are always respectful.

1. How to Conjugate

Group 1: U-Verbs (Godan)

Change the final "u" sound to the corresponding "i" sound, then add ます.

  • 買かう (to buy) → 買かいます
  • 待まつ (to wait) → 待まちます
  • 行いく (to go) → 行いきます
  • 飲のむ (to drink) → 飲のみます

Group 2: Ru-Verbs (Ichidan)

Simply remove る and add ます.

  • 食たべる (to eat) → 食たべます
  • 見みる (to see) → 見みます
  • 寝ねる (to sleep) → 寝ねます

Group 3: Irregular Verbs

  • する (to do) → します
  • 来くる (to come) → 来きます

2. Verb Tenses (Polite)

Tense Ending Example (食たべる)
Present / Future ます 食たべます
Negative ません 食たべません
Past Affirmative ました 食たべました
Past Negative ませんでした 食たべませんでした

3. Important Tip: The Verb Stem

The part before "masu" (e.g., 買かい, 食たべ) is called the Verb Stem. This stem is a very important building block for more advanced grammar points!
