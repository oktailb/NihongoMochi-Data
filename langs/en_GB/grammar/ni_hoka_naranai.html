Nothing But: ～にほかならない

When you want to identify the absolute, single truth behind a situation, we use `～にほかならない`. It means "it is nothing other than" or "it is purely because of." It eliminates all other possibilities to highlight one specific cause or identity.

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun / [Verb/Adj Plain Form] + こと` + にほかならない

2. Absolute Examples

  • 彼かれの成功せいこうは努力どりょくの結果けっか`にほかならない`。
    (His success is nothing other than the result of his efforts.)
  • これは愛あい`にほかならない`。
    (This is nothing but love.)
  • 戦争せんそうは悲劇ひげき`にほかならない`。
    (War is nothing but a tragedy.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a formal and very strong expression. It's often used in essays, speeches, or when making a profound philosophical point. It says "Don't look anywhere else; this is the true essence of the matter!"
