It's No Use: ～てもしょうがない / ～てもしかたがない

Sometimes, worrying or acting is just a waste of energy because the situation won't change. `～てもしょうがない` (and its more formal cousin `～てもしかたがない`) is used to say "there's no point in..." or "it's no use...-ing."

1. Sentence Structure

`[Verb/Adj Te-form] + もしょうがない / もしかたがない`

2. Realistic Examples

  • 済すんだことを`後悔こうかいしてもしょうがない`。
    (It's no use regretting what is already done.)
  • 今いまさら`急いそいでもしかたがない`。欲よくっくり行いこう。
    (There's no point in rushing now. Let's go slowly.)
  • 文句もんくを`言いってもしょうがない`よ。自分じぶんでやるしかない。
    (It's no use complaining. You just have to do it yourself.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a practical and somewhat stoic expression. It's about accepting reality and moving on. While it might sound a bit negative, it's actually used to encourage someone to stop wasting time on things they can't control and focus on what they `can` do.
