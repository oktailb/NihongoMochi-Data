The Particle よ (Emphasis and New Information)

The particle よ is placed at the end of a sentence to provide emphasis. It is used when the speaker is sharing new information, giving a warning, or expressing a strong opinion.

1. Sharing New Information

Use よ when you believe the listener doesn't know what you are saying.

  • この本ほんはおもしろいですよ。
    (This book is interesting, you know!)
  • 明日あしたは休やすみですよ。
    (Tomorrow is a holiday, just so you know.)

2. Giving a Warning or Advice

It can be used to catch the listener's attention or give them a friendly reminder.

  • あぶないですよ！
    (It's dangerous!)
  • バスが来きましたよ。
    (The bus is here!)

3. Expressing a Strong Opinion

Adding よ shows that the speaker is confident in what they are saying.

  • 私わたしは行いきませんよ。
    (I'm definitely not going.)

4. よ vs ね

While ね (right?) seeks agreement, よ (you know!) asserts information. They are opposites in terms of "shared knowledge."

  • ね: We both know this.
  • よ: I am telling you this because you might not know.
