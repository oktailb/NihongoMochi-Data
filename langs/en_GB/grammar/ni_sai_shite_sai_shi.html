On the occasion of: ～に際して / ～に際し

The expression `～に際して` (にさいして) is used to indicate a specific time or occasion when something important is about to happen. it is a very formal expression, often used in ceremonies, official speeches, or written notices.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `に際して` directly to a `Noun` or the `Dictionary Form` of a verb. The shorter version `に際し` is even more formal.

`[Noun / Verb Dictionary Form] + に際して`

  • 試験しけんを受うける`に際して`、注意点ちゅういてんを説明せつめいします。
    (On the occasion of taking the exam, I will explain the points to note.)
  • 新あたらしい事業じぎょうを始はじめる`に際し`、一言ひとことごあいさつを申もうし上あげます。
    (On the occasion of starting a new business, I would like to say a few words.)
  • 帰国きこく`に際して`、お世話せわになった人ひとたちにプレゼントを渡わたした。
    (Upon returning to my country, I gave presents to the people who took care of me.)

2. Key Nuance: Important Events

Unlike the general `~とき　に`, `～に際して` is reserved for non-recurring, significant life events or formal public occasions. It often introduces an action performed in preparation for or as part of that event.

3. Distinction from ～にあたって

Both are formal and used for occasions, but `～に際して` is often more "point-in-time" focused, while `～にあたって` often carries a nuance of "facing a new stage" or intentional preparation.
