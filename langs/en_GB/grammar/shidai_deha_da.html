Dependent Outcomes: ～次第だ / ～次第で（は）

Life is full of "it depends"! `～次第 (しだい)` is used to show that an outcome depends entirely on a specific factor. It also has a second life meaning "as soon as," but in N2/N3, we focus on the "dependency" aspect.

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Noun` + 次第だ (It depends on X)
  • `Noun` + 次第で（は） (Depending on X, Y might happen)
  • `Verb Stem` + 次第 (As soon as X is done...)

2. Dynamic Examples

  • 努力どりょく`次第だ`。
    (It depends on your effort.)
  • 試験しけんの結果けっか`次第では`、卒業そつぎょうできないこともある。
    (Depending on the exam results, one might not be able to graduate.)
  • 準備じゅんびができ`次第`、出発しゅっぱつします。
    (As soon as the preparations are ready, we will depart.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very logical word! `～次第だ` at the end of a sentence identifies the "controlling factor." `～次第では` is used to introduce a specific possibility that depends on Factor X. It shows that the future is not yet fixed!
