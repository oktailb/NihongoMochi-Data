Covered in Mess: ～だらけ

When something is completely covered in something unpleasant or unwanted, we use `～だらけ`. It's almost always used for things you `don't` want to see, like mistakes, mud, or debt!

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + だらけ

2. Messy Examples

  • この作文さくぶんは間違まちがい`だらけ`だ。
    (This essay is full of mistakes.)
  • 泥どろ`だらけ`の靴くつで家いえに上あらないで！
    (Don't come into the house with shoes covered in mud!)
  • 借金しゃっきん`だらけ`の生活せいかつは苦くるしい。
    (Life full of debt is painful.)

3. Sensei's Note

Be careful! Don't use `～だらけ` for good things. You wouldn't say "a room full of presents" with this. For neutral or positive "fullness," we use `～でいっぱい`. `～だらけ` is for the dirty, the messy, and the "oh no" moments!
