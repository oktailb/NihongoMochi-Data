The Particle の (Possession and Connection)

The particle の is used to connect two nouns. It most commonly indicates possession (like "'s" in English), but it can also describe one noun with another.

1. Possession (A's B)

In the structure [Noun A] の [Noun B], Noun B belongs to Noun A.

  • 私わたしの本ほん
    (My book / The book of me)
  • 田中たなかさんの車くるま
    (Mr. Tanaka's car)

2. Connection / Description

It is also used to show which category or origin a noun belongs to.

  • 日本にほんの車くるま
    (Japanese car / Car of Japan)
  • 日本語にほんごの先生せんせい
    (Japanese language teacher)
  • 大学だいがくの学生がくせい
    (University student)

3. Pronoun Replacement

の can sometimes act as the word "one" to avoid repeating a noun.

  • 「これは誰だれのかばんですか？」
    「私わたしのです。」
    ("Whose bag is this?" "It is mine [my one].")
