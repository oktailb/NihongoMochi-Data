"Even if": ～ても

The conjunction ～ても is used to express a condition under which something happens regardless. It translates to "even if" or "even though."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach も to the Te-form of verbs, adjectives, or nouns.

  • Verbs: 雨あめが降ふっても、行いきます。
    (Even if it rains, I will go.)
  • い-Adj: 高たかくても、買かいたいです。
    (Even if it's expensive, I want to buy it.)
  • な-Adj / Nouns: 嫌きらいでも、食たべなければなりません。
    (Even if you hate it, you must eat it.)

2. Common Question Phrase: ～てもいいですか

This structure is the base for asking permission (May I...?).

  • 入はいってもいいですか。(Is it okay if I enter?)
