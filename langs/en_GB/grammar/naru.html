To Become: ～なる

The verb `なる` (to become) is used to describe a change in state. The way it connects depends on whether you are using a noun or an adjective.

1. With Nouns and Na-Adjectives

Add the particle `に` before `なる`.

  • 将来しょうらい、医者いしゃ`になります`。
    (I will become a doctor in the future.)
  • 町まちが静しずか`になりました`。
    (The town became quiet.)

2. With I-Adjectives

Replace the final `～い` with `～く` before `なる`.

  • 暗くらい → `暗くらくなる`
    空そらが`暗くらくなりました`。
    (The sky became dark.)
  • 寒さむい → `寒さむくなる`
    これから`寒さむくなります`。
    (It will become cold from now on.)
