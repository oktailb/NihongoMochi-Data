Goal of Movement: ～に行く / ～に来る

This expression is used to say that you "go" or "come" `in order to` perform a certain action. It connects a purpose with a verb of movement.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach the particle `に` to the `Verb Stem` (the part before `~ます`), followed by a movement verb like `行く` (go), `来る` (come), or `帰る` (return).

`[Verb Stem] + に + [Movement Verb]`

  • スーパーへ買かいものに`行いきます`。
    (I am going to the supermarket to shop.)
  • 図書館としょかんへ勉強べんきょうしに`行いきました`。
    (I went to the library to study.)
  • うちに遊あそびに`来こてください`。
    (Please come to my house to play/hang out.)

2. Using with Nouns

If the action is already a noun (like "shopping" or "study"), you can attach `に` directly to it without `する`.

  • デパートへ買かいもの`に`行いきます。
    (I go for shopping.)

3. Visualizing the Structure

The `に` acts as a target marker for your intention. You are moving `towards` the action.
