Based on: ～をもとに（して）

The expression ～をもとに（して） is used when something is created or developed using something else as a basis, raw material, or inspiration. It translates to "based on...", "from...", or "taking... as a basis."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach をもとに（して） directly to a Noun. To modify a following noun, use をもとにした.

[Noun] + をもとに（して）

  • この映画えいがは実話じつわをもとにしています。
    (This movie is based on a true story.)
  • ひらがなは漢字かんじをもとに作つくられました。
    (Hiragana was created from [based on] Kanji.)
  • テストの結果けっかをもとにしたクラス分わけを行おこないます。
    (We will conduct class grouping based on test results.)

2. Key Usage: Creative Origins

It is often used when talking about books, movies, designs, or systems that have a clear source of inspiration or material.

3. Distinction from ～に基づいて

  • ～をもとに: Focuses on the raw material or source (e.g., a story based on a fact).
  • ～に基づいて: Focuses on a formal basis, rule, or principle (e.g., acting according to the law).
