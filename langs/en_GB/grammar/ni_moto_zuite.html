Based on the Facts: ～に基づいて (にもとづいて)

Every great story or theory needs a foundation! `～に基づいて` (written as `～に基づいて`) means "based on" or "on the basis of." It identifies the source material, data, or principles that an action or idea is built upon.

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + に基づいて / に基づく

2. Foundational Examples

  • この映画えいがは事実じじつ`に基づいて`作つくられています。
    (This movie is based on a true story.)
  • 調査ちょうさの結果けっか`に基づいて`計画けいかくを立てる。
    (To make a plan based on the results of the investigation.)
  • この本ほんは実体験じったいけん`に基づく`小説しょうせつだ。
    (This book is a novel based on real-life experience.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very solid and objective expression. It shows that there is a logical or factual "floor" beneath what you're saying. Use it in formal reports, when discussing research, or when justifying a decision with evidence!
