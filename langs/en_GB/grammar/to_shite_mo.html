Even if we Assume: ～としても

Sometimes even a possibility doesn't change the outcome. ～としても is used to say "even assuming X is true, Y is still the case." it's a way to acknowledge a possibility but dismiss its impact on your conclusion.

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb/Adj/Noun Plain Form] + としても

2. Resolute Examples

  • 行いけるとしても、だいぶ遅おそくなると思おもう。
    (Even if I can go, I think I'll be quite late.)
  • それが本当ほんとうだとしても、彼かれの味方みかたをします。
    (Even if that is true, I will still take his side.)
  • 忙いそがしいとしても、連絡れんらくぐらいはできるはずだ。
    (Even if you're busy, you should at least be able to get in touch.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is the "Stubborn Hypothesis." It's very similar to tatoe ~te mo, but ～としても focuses more on the logical assumption of X being true. It says "Even if we accept X as a fact, it doesn't change the Y result." It's a very firm way to argue a point!
