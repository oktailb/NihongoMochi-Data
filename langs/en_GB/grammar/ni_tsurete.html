As... / Along with: ～につれて

The expression `～につれて` is used to describe a change that happens simultaneously with another change. As one thing moves or evolves, another thing also changes in a corresponding way.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `につれて` to the `Dictionary Form` of a verb (representing change) or to a `Noun` (representing a process).

`[Change A] につれて [Change B]`

  • 台風たいふうが近ちかづく`につれて`、風かぜが強つよくなってきた。
    (`As` the typhoon approaches, the wind is becoming stronger.)
  • 時間じかんが経たつ`につれて`、悲かなしみが消きえていった。
    (`As` time passes, the sadness has been fading away.)
  • 年としをとる`につれて`、体からだが弱よわくなります。
    (`As` you get older, your body becomes weaker.)

2. Key Nuance: Natural Progression

It describes a natural, often uncontrollable progression where B happens as a side effect of A. The verbs used must express some kind of movement or change (e.g., getting older, rising, approaching).

3. Comparison: ～につれて vs ～にしたがって

  • `～につれて:` Focuses on the natural, simultaneous progression in one direction.
  • `～にしたがって:` Can also imply following a rule, an order, or a more complex plan.
