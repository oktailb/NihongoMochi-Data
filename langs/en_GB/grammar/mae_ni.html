Before Doing: ～前に

To say "before doing something" or "before a certain event," Japanese uses the word `前まえに`.

1. Using with Verbs

Always use the `Dictionary Form` of the verb, regardless of whether the overall sentence is past, present, or future.

`[Verb Dictionary Form] + 前まえに`

  • 寝ねる`前まえに`、本ほんを読よみます。
    (Before sleeping, I read a book.)
  • 日本にほんへ行いく`前まえに`、漢字かんじを勉強べんきょうしました。
    (Before going to Japan, I studied Kanji.)

2. Using with Nouns

When using a noun (an event or time), connect it using the particle `の`.

`[Noun] + の + 前まえに`

  • 食事しょくじ`の前まえに`、手てを洗あらいます。
    (Before the meal, I wash my hands.)
  • テスト`の前まえに`、復習ふくしゅうしました。
    (Before the test, I reviewed.)

3. Important Note

The verb before `前に` is always in the dictionary form, even if the second action happened in the past. The tense of the whole sentence is decided by the `final` verb.
