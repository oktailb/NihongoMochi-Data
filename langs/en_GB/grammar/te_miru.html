Trying to do: ～てみる

The expression `～てみる` is used to say that you "try to do something" or "do something to see what happens." It often implies a lighthearted attempt or a test.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `みる` to the `Te-form` of a verb.

  • おいしそうですね。食たべて`みます`。
    (It looks delicious. I'll `try` eating it.)
  • この靴くつを履はいて`みてもいいですか`。
    (May I `try` on these shoes?)
  • わからないので、先生せんせいに聞きいて`みました`。
    (Since I didn't know, I `tried` asking the teacher.)

2. Difference from "Try hard" (努力する)

`～てみる` is for testing or experiencing something. For making a serious effort, Japanese uses other expressions like `～ようとする` or `努力する`.

3. Conjugation

Once `みる` is attached, it conjugates like a Group 2 (Ichidan) verb.

  • `Polite:` ～てみます
  • `Past:` ～てみました
  • `Negative:` ～てみない
