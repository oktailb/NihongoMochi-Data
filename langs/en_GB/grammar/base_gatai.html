Difficult to do: ～がたい

The suffix `～がたい` is attached to the `~ます stem` of a verb to express that something is "difficult to do."

1. Pedagogical Note

Unlike `～にくい` (which refers to physical or technical difficulty), `～がたい` expresses a `psychological or emotional difficulty`. It is used when there is an internal resistance or when something feels "almost impossible" due to the nature of the situation.

`Teacher's Tip:` It is most commonly used with verbs of perception or mental state, such as "to believe," "to forgive," or "to imagine."

2. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (~ます stem) + `がたい`

3. Examples

  • それは信しんじ`がたい`話はなしだ。
    (That is a story that is hard to believe [unbelievable].)
  • この罪つみは許ゆるし`がたい`。
    (This crime is hard to forgive [unforgivable].)
  • 彼かれの行動こうどうは理解りかいし`がたい`。
    (His behavior is difficult to understand.)
