Basic Particles (助詞じょし) - Summary

Particles are the "glue" of the Japanese language. They follow words to indicate their grammatical role in a sentence.

1. The Topic: は

Identifies what the sentence is about (pronounced wa).

  • 私わたしは学生がくせいです。
    (As for me, I am a student.)

2. The Subject: が

Points the spotlight at the specific performer of an action.

  • 猫ねこがいます。
    (There is a cat.)

3. The Direct Object: を

Marks the thing being acted upon (pronounced o).

  • 水みずを飲のみます。
    (I drink water.)

4. Destination and Time: に

Indicates where you are going or a specific point in time.

  • 日本にほんに行いきます。
    (I go to Japan.)
  • 三時さんじに会あいます。
    (I will meet at 3 o'clock.)

5. Location of Action and Means: で

Indicates where an action happens or the tool used.

  • 学校がっこうで勉強べんきょうします。
    (I study at school.)
  • ハシで食たべます。
    (I eat with chopsticks.)

6. Possession and Connection: の

Connects two nouns (like "'s" or "of").

  • 私わたしの本ほん
    (My book)
