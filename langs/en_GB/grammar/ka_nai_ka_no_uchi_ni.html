In the Blink of an Eye: ～か～ないかのうちに

When an action happens so quickly after another that they almost seem to overlap, we use ～か～ないかのうちに. It translates to "no sooner had... than..." or "just as..." It emphasizes the extreme speed of the sequence.

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb Dictionary Form] + か + [Verb Nai Form] + かのうちに

Note: The same verb is used in both forms.

2. Lightning-Fast Examples

  • ベルが鳴なるか鳴ならないかのうちに、彼かれは教室きょうしつを出でた。
    (No sooner had the bell rung than he left the classroom.)
  • 布団ふとんに入はいるか入はいらないかのうちに、眠ねむってしまった。
    (Just as I got into bed, I fell fast asleep.)
  • デパートのドアが開あくか開あかないかのうちに、客きゃくがなだれ込こんだ。
    (No sooner had the department store doors opened than the customers rushed in.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very dramatic expression! It conveys that the second action happened practically at the same moment as the first. Use it when you want to paint a picture of sudden, rapid movement or change.
