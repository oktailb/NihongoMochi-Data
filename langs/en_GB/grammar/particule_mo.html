The Particle も (Too, Also)

The particle `も` is used to say "also", "too", or "as well". It replaces or combines with other particles to show that a similar situation applies to another topic or object.

1. Basic Usage (Replacing は or を)

When `も` is used, it completely replaces the particles は or を.

  • 私わたしは学生がくせいです。田中たなかさん`も`学生がくせいです。
    (I am a student. Mr. Tanaka is `also` a student.)
  • りんごを食たべます。蜜柑みかん`も`食たべます。
    (I eat apples. I `also` eat mandarins.)

2. Emphasizing "Both" or "Neither"

By using `も` twice, you can express "A and B" or "Neither A nor B" (when used with a negative verb).

  • 犬いぬ`も`猫ねこ`も`好すきです。
    (I like `both` dogs and cats.)
  • ペン`も`紙かみ`も`ありません。
    (There is `neither` a pen nor paper.)

3. Usage with Other Particles

Unlike は and を, `も` does not replace particles like に or で; it is added after them.

  • 日本にほんに行いきました。韓国かんこく`にも`行いきました。
    (I went to Japan. I `also` went to Korea.)
