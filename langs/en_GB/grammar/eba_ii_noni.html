If Only / You Should Have: ～ればいいのに

We use this to express regret, a missed opportunity, or to give a slightly critical suggestion. It's that feeling of "If only X happened, things would be better."

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb Conditionnel -eba] + いいのに

2. Emotional Examples

  • もっと早はやく寝ねればいいのに。
    (You should really go to bed earlier [but you don't].)
  • もっと安やすければいいのになあ。
    (If only it were cheaper [but it's not].)
  • 彼かれも来くればいいのに。
    (I wish he would come, too [but he isn't here].)

3. Sensei's Note

The のに at the end adds a sense of regret or lingering dissatisfaction. It implies that the actual situation is different from the ideal one. If you want to give a simple, nice suggestion without the regret, just use ～たらいいですよ.
