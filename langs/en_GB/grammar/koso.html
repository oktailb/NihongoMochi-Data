Ultimate Emphasis: ～こそ

When you want to point your finger at one specific thing and say "This is the one! Nothing else!", we use ～こそ. It's a particle of strong focus, often used for determination, gratitude, or identifying a true cause.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + こそ

2. Powerful Examples

  • こちらこそ、よろしくお願ねがいします。
    (It is I [not you] who should be asking for your favor! - Standard polite reply)
  • 来年らいねんこそ、日本にほんへ行いくぞ！
    (Next year for sure [unlike previous years], I will go to Japan!)
  • 愛情あいじょうがあるからこそ、厳きびしくするのです。
    (It is precisely because there is love that I am being strict.)

3. Sensei's Note

こそ is like a spotlight. It dismisses other possibilities and shines brightly on the noun it follows. You'll often see it in ～からこそ (precisely because) or when returning a greeting to show that the other person is the one who deserves the thanks or the honor.
