The Particle が (Subject Marker)

The particle が identifies the subject of a sentence. While は (topic) sets the stage, が points a spotlight at the specific performer of an action or the possessor of a quality.

1. The "Spotlight" Function

Use が when you are introducing new information or when you want to emphasize who or what is doing something.

2. Key Examples

  • 雨あめが降ふっています。
    (It is raining. / Rain is falling.)
  • 誰だれが来きましたか？
    (Who came?)
  • 私わたしが行いきます。
    (I will go. / Emphasis on 'I')

3. Expressing Existence and Ability

The particle が is almost always used with verbs of existence (ある, いる) and verbs of ability/potential.

  • 猫ねこがいます。
    (There is a cat.)
  • 日本語にほんごが分わかります。
    (I understand Japanese.)

4. が vs は (Quick Tip)

In the sentence 「空そらは青あおい」, we are talking about the sky in general. In 「空そらが青あおい」, we are specifically pointing out that the sky (right now, look!) is blue.
