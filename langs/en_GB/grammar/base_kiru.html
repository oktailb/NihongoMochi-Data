Doing to the end: ～きる

The suffix `～きる` (derived from the verb `きる`, to cut) indicates that an action is performed `completely`, `definitively`, or `to the very end`.

1. Two Main Meanings

1. `Completion:` Finishing something entirely until nothing remains.
2. `Intensity/Emphasis:` Doing something with great effort or reaching a state of exhaustion.

2. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (~ます stem) + `きる`
  • Negative form: `～きれない` (unable to finish completely / too much to handle)

3. Examples

  • こんなにたくさんの料理りょうりは食たべ`きれません`。
    (I can't finish all this food [there is too much of it].)
  • マラソンを走はしり`きった`。
    (I ran the marathon all the way to the end.)
  • 長ながい時間じかん働はたらいて、疲つかれ`きって`しまった。
    (After working for a long time, I am completely exhausted.)
