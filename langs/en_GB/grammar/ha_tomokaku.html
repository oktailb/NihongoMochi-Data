Setting Aside X: ～はともかく

When you want to focus on a more important issue and put another detail aside for a moment, we use `～はともかく`. It means "X aside," or "Regardless of X, the real point is Y."

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun` + はともかく

2. Practical Examples

  • 値段ねだん`はともかく`、デザインはとてもいい。
    (Setting the price aside, the design is excellent.)
  • 勝敗しょうはい`はともかく`、一生懸命いっしょうけんめい頑張がんばることが大切たいせつだ。
    (Setting aside winning or losing, trying your hardest is what's important.)
  • 料理りょうりができるか`はともかく`、食たべるのは好すきだ。
    (Whether I can cook or not aside, I love to eat!)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a great way to prioritize information. It doesn't mean the first thing isn't true; it just means it's not the priority right now. It's often used when Y is much more certain or important than X.
