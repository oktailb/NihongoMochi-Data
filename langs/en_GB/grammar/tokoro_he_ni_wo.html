Caught in the Moment: ～ところ（へ・に・を）

Timing is everything! ～ところ (written as ～ところ) describes a specific point in time relative to an action. The particles he, ni, and wo that follow it change the nuance based on how the second action affects the first.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (Dictionary/Continuous/Past Form) + ところ（へ・に・を）

2. Timely Examples

  • 出でかけようとしたところへ、電話でんわがかかってきた。
    (Just as I was about to go out, the phone rang.)
  • いいところに来きたね。ちょうど今いまから食たべるところだ。
    (You've come at a good time. We're just about to eat.)
  • 隠かくれてお菓子かしを食たべているところを見みつかった。
    (I was caught right while I was secretly eating sweets.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is all about the "snapshot." ところを is often used for being "caught in the act." ところに/へ is for when a second event happens and affects the first one. It adds a dramatic, cinematic feel to your descriptions of events!
