Accompanying Change: ～に伴って (にともなって)

In life, when one big thing changes, other things usually change with it! `～に伴って` (written as `～に伴って`) means "accompanying," "along with," or "as a result of." it describes two changes happening simultaneously.

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun / [Verb Dictionary Form] + の` + に伴って

2. Sequential Examples

  • 人口じんこうの増加ぞうか`に伴って`、ゴミの問題もんだいも深刻になった。
    (Along with the increase in population, the waste problem has also become serious.)
  • 時代じだいの変化へんか`に伴って`、考かんがえ方かたも変わる。
    (As the times change, ways of thinking also change.)
  • 携帯電話けいたいでんわの普及ふきゅう`に伴い`、便利べんりになった。
    (With the spread of mobile phones, life has become more convenient.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of this as "Action and Reaction." While `~に　つれて` is for a natural, gradual progression, `～に伴って` often emphasizes that the second change is a logical or necessary consequence of the first. It's used for large-scale social or physical phenomena.
