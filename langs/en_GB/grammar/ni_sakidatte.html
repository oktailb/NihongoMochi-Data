Prior to Action: ～に先立って (にさきだって)

Success favors the prepared! `～に先立って` (written as `～に先立って`) is a formal way to say "prior to" or "before." It is used when one action must happen as a preparation or a precursor to a major event.

1. Sentence Structure

`Noun / Verb (Dictionary Form)` + に先立って

2. Preparatory Examples

  • 開店かいてん`に先立って`、セレモニーが行われた。
    (Prior to the store's opening, a ceremony was held.)
  • 試験しけんを始はじめる`に先立って`、注意事項ちゅういじこうを説明せつめいします。
    (Prior to starting the exam, I will explain the points of caution.)
  • 新あたらしい事業じぎょうを始はじめる`に先立って`、市場調査しじょうちょうさをする。
    (Prior to starting a new business, we will conduct market research.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is much more formal than `まえ　に`. It's used for important events like weddings, business launches, or official ceremonies. It highlights that the first action is a significant step that leads into the second, larger event.
