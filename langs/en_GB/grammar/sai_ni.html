Formal Timing: ～際に (さいに)

In polite and professional situations, "when" becomes `～際に`. It's the formal equivalent of `とき　に`. It's used when describing an action that happens on a specific occasion or at a particular time.

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Verb (Dictionary/Past Form)` + 際に
  • `Noun` + の際に

2. Professional Examples

  • お帰かえりの`際に`は、お忘わすれ物もののないようご注意ちゅういください。
    (When you leave [at the time of departure], please be careful not to leave anything behind.)
  • 申もうし込こむ`際に`は、印鑑いんかんが必要ひつようです。
    (When applying, a seal is required.)
  • 来日らいにちした`際に`、首相しゅしょうに会あった。
    (When he visited Japan, he met the Prime Minister.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of this as the "Red Carpet" version of `とき`. You'll see it in manuals, instructions, and hear it in formal announcements. It sounds very respectful and clear. Use it when you want to sound professional and organized!
