Formal Timing: ～際に (さいに)

In polite and professional situations, "when" becomes ～際に. It's the formal equivalent of toki ni. It's used when describing an action that happens on a specific occasion or at a particular time.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Verb (Dictionary/Past Form) + 際に
  • Noun + の際に

2. Professional Examples

  • お帰かえりの際には、お忘わすれ物もののないようご注意ちゅういください。
    (When you leave [at the time of departure], please be careful not to leave anything behind.)
  • 申もうし込こむ際には、印鑑いんかんが必要ひつようです。
    (When applying, a seal is required.)
  • 来日らいにちした際に、首相しゅしょうに会あった。
    (When he visited Japan, he met the Prime Minister.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of this as the "Red Carpet" version of toki. You'll see it in manuals, instructions, and hear it in formal announcements. It sounds very respectful and clear. Use it when you want to sound professional and organized!
