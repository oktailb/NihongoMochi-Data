The Last Time: ～たきり

Sometimes an action happens once, and then... nothing else happens ever again. ～たきり is used to describe a state that has continued ever since a specific past event. It carries the nuance of "that was the last time."

1. Sentence Structure

[Verb Ta-form] + きり

2. Lingering Examples

  • 彼かれとは、去年きょねん会あったきりだ。
    (I haven't seen him since we met last year [that was the last time].)
  • 本ほんを借かりたきり、返かえしていない。
    (I borrowed the book and haven't returned it since.)
  • 座すわったきり、一歩いっぽも動こうとしない。
    (Since he sat down, he hasn't moved a single step.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is very similar to ~te irai, but ～たきり often focuses on the fact that an expected follow-up action (like returning a book or meeting again) has not happened. It emphasizes the "frozen" nature of the situation since that one moment in the past.
