Listing Actions: ～たり ～たりする

The structure ～たり ～たりする is used to list two or more actions as examples among others. It implies that you do things like A and B, but not necessarily only A and B.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach り to the Ta-form of verbs. The final action is always followed by する.

[Verb A た形たけい] + り、[Verb B た形たけい] + り + する

  • 日曜日にちようびは、本ほんを読よんだり、テレビを見みたりします。
    (On Sundays, I do things like read books and watch TV.)
  • 昨日きのうは、買かいものをしたり、散歩さんぽをしたりしました。
    (Yesterday, I did things like shopping and taking a walk.)

2. Difference from ～て

  • ～て: Describes a specific sequence of actions (I did A, then B, then C).
  • ～たり: Describes representative examples of actions, in no particular order.

3. With Adjectives

You can also use this with adjectives to describe a state that fluctuates.

  • 最近さいきんは、暑あつかったり、寒さむかったりします。
    (Lately, it's sometimes hot and sometimes cold.)
