Unmatched Expertise: ～にかけては

Everyone has a special talent! When you want to say "When it comes to X, no one is better than them," we use ～にかけては. It defines a specific field or skill where someone truly shines.

1. Sentence Structure

Noun + にかけては

2. Expert Examples

  • 漢字かんじの知識ちしきにかけては、彼かれに勝かてる人ひとはいない。
    (When it comes to knowledge of Kanji, no one can beat him.)
  • 足あしの速はやさにかけては、彼かれがクラスで一番だ。
    (When it comes to speed, he is the fastest in the class.)
  • 料理りょうりの味あじにかけては、この店みせが最高さいこうだ。
    (When it comes to the taste of the food, this shop is the best.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a high compliment! It marks the boundaries of someone's "kingdom." Within that specific area (X), they are the master. It's much more focused than just saying someone is "good at everything."
