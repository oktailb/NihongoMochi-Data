On One Hand...: ～一方で（は）

Life is full of contrasts. When you want to describe two different sides of a situation, or two actions happening at the same time by the same person, we use `～一方で（は）`. It means "on the other hand" or "at the same time."

1. Sentence Structure

  • `Verb (Plain Form)` + 一方で
  • `い-Adj` + 一方で
  • `な-Adj` + な / である + 一方で
  • `Noun` + である + 一方で

2. Balanced Examples

  • 彼かれは歌手かしゅとして活躍かつやくする`一方で`、小説しょうせつも書かいている。
    (While he is active as a singer, on the other hand, he also writes novels.)
  • この仕事しごとは給料きゅうりょうが高たかい`一方で`、とても忙いそがしい。
    (While this job has a high salary, on the other hand, it's very busy.)
  • 子供こどもが生うまれてうれしい`一方で`、不安ふあんもある。
    (On one hand I'm happy the baby was born, but on the other, I feel anxious.)

3. Sensei's Note

Think of this as a scale. You are putting one fact on one side and a contrasting or additional fact on the other. It's often used to show that something isn't just "all good" or "all bad," but a mix of both!
