After Doing: ～てから

The expression `～てから` is used to describe an action that takes place immediately after another action is completed. It translates to "after doing..." or "since doing..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `から` to the `Te-form` of a verb.

`[Verb て形てけい] + から、[Main Action]`

  • 手てを`洗あらってから`、食たべます。
    (After washing my hands, I eat.)
  • 宿題しゅくだいを`してから`、ゲームをしました。
    (After doing my homework, I played a game.)

2. Nuance: Prerequisite

Unlike `～あとで`, `～てから` often implies that the first action is a necessary step or a prerequisite for the second action to happen.

  • よく`考かんがえてから`、言いってください。
    (Please think carefully `before` [and then] speaking.)

3. Time Since an Event

It can also be used to say "since [an event] happened."

  • 日本にほんに`来くてから`、三ヶ月さんかげつになります。
    (It has been three months since I came to Japan.)
