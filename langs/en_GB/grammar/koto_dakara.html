Predicting Behavior: ～ことだから

We use this when we can predict someone's actions because we know their character so well. It means "Precisely because it's [Person], I'm sure that..." It's like saying "Typical of X, he will surely..."

1. Sentence Structure

Noun (Person) + のことだから

2. Character-Driven Examples

  • 彼かれのことだから、また遅刻ちこくするだろう。
    (Knowing him [typical of him], he'll probably be late again.)
  • まじめな田中たなかさんのことだから、もう準備じゅんびはできているはずだ。
    (Since it's the serious Tanaka-san, I'm sure the preparations are already done.)
  • おしゃべりな彼女かのじょのことだから、もうみんなに話はなしたに違ちがいない。
    (Knowing how talkative she is, she must have told everyone already.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very common way to show you are familiar with someone's habits. It sets up a logical expectation based on personality. Usually, it's followed by a guess (~darou, ~hazu da, ~ni chigai nai). It shows a deep understanding of the person you're talking about!
