With all one's heart: ～をこめて

The expression ～をこめて (written as ～を込めて) is used to say that an action is performed with a strong feeling, emotion, or prayer. It translates to "with all my...", "filled with...", or "putting my [emotion] into..."

1. Sentence Structure

Attach をこめて directly to a Noun representing a feeling or abstract concept.

[Feeling Noun] + をこめて

  • 心こころをこめて、プレゼントを選えらびました。
    (I chose the present with all my heart.)
  • 感謝かんしゃの気持きもちをこめて、手紙てがみを書かきました。
    (I wrote a letter filled with feelings of gratitude.)
  • 平和へいわへの祈いのりをこめて、歌うたいます。
    (I will sing putting my prayers for peace [into the song].)

2. Common Nouns used with ～をこめて

Noun Meaning
愛あい Love
心こころ Heart
感謝かんしゃ Gratitude
祈いのり Prayer

3. Nuance

It implies that the emotion is the driving force behind the action. It is a very warm and sincere expression.
