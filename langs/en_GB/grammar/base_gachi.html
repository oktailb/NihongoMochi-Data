Tendency: ～がち

The suffix `～がち` is used to express a tendency to do something or a state that happens frequently. It is most often used for `negative tendencies` or undesirable situations.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `がち` to the `Verb Stem` or directly to a `Noun`.

`[Verb Stem / Noun] + がち`

  • 最近さいきん、若わかい人ひとは野菜やさいが不足ふそく`しがち`です。
    (Recently, young people tend to lack vegetables [in their diet].)
  • 一人暮ひとりぐらしは病気びょうき`に``なりがち`です。
    (People living alone tend to get sick more often.)
  • 冬ふゆは曇くもり`がち`な天気てんきが多おおいです。
    (In winter, the weather tends to be cloudy.)

2. Common Expressions

Expression Meaning
忘わすれがち Tend to forget
遅おくれがち Often late
病気びょうきがち Prone to illness
留守るすがち Often away from home

3. Nuance

Unlike `～やすい` (which describes an inherent quality), `～がち` focuses on the `frequency` of a negative event happening more often than it should.
