Asking for Advice: ～たらいいですか

The expression `～たらいいですか` is used when you are seeking advice, suggestions, or instructions from someone. It translates to "What should I do?" or "What would be good to...?"

1. Sentence Structure

It is often used with question words like `どこ` (where), `いつ` (when), or `どう` (how).

`[Question Word] + [Verb ～たら] + いいですか`

  • どこで買かっ`たらいいですか`。
    (Where should I buy [it]?)
  • 頭あたまが痛いたいんですが、どうし`たらいいですか`。
    (I have a headache; what should I do?)
  • いつ出だし`たらいいですか`。
    (When should I submit [it]?)

2. Difference from ～なければなりません

  • `～なければなりません:` Focuses on a strict obligation (What must I do?).
  • `～たらいいですか:` Focuses on seeking the `best` or most `convenient` option (What is the best thing to do?).

3. Formal Variation

In very formal situations, use `～たらよろしいでしょうか`.
