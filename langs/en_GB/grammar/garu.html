Observing Others' Emotions: ～がる

In Japanese, you generally cannot state another person's feelings or desires directly (e.g., "He is cold"). Instead, you must say that they "appear" to feel that way. The suffix ～がる is used for this purpose.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach がる to the Adjective Stem (remove the final ~i or ~na).

  • かれは寒さむがっています。
    (He appears to be cold / He is acting cold.)
  • 子供こどもがプレゼントを欲ほしがっています。
    (The child [appears to] want a present.)
  • 恥はずかがらないでください。
    (Please don't act shy / don't be embarrassed.)

2. Common Adjectives used with ～がる

Adjective With ～がる Meaning
寒さむい (Cold) 寒さむがる To feel/act cold
怖こわい (Scary) 怖こわがる To be afraid
欲ほしい (Want) 欲ほしがる To want something
～たい (Want to do) ～たがる To want to do

3. Important Note

Using ～がる turns the adjective into a Group 1 verb. It is almost always used in the ～ている form to describe a currently observable state.
