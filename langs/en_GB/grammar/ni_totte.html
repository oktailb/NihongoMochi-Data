Perspective: ～にとって

The expression `～にとって` is used to say "from the perspective of..." or "for...". It indicates that the following statement is true or a certain evaluation exists when viewed from that person's standpoint.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `にとって` directly to a `Noun` (usually a person, organization, or group).

`[Noun] + にとって`

  • 私わたし`にとって`、家族かぞくが一番いちばん大切たいせつです。
    (For me, family is the most important thing.)
  • 留学生りゅうがくせい`にとって`、漢字かんじは難むずかしいです。
    (For foreign students, Kanji is difficult.)
  • この計画けいかくは、わが社しゃ`にとって`とても重要じゅうようです。
    (This plan is very important for our company.)

2. Nuance: Evaluation

It is often followed by adjectives that show value or evaluation, such as `important`, `difficult`, `necessary`, or `good/bad`.

3. Distinction from ～に（対して）

While `~に` can sometimes mean "to/for", `～にとって` specifically highlights the `point of view` or the `impact` on that person. It doesn't mean you are doing something `to` them, but rather how they perceive something.
