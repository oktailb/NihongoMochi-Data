When / At the time: ～時に

The expression ～時に (ときに) is used to indicate the time or occasion when something happens. It is the Japanese equivalent of the word "when."

1. Sentence Structure

It connects to verbs, adjectives, and nouns using their Plain Form (Dictionary, Nai, Ta). For nouns, add の.

  • 子供こどものときに、よく泳およぎました。
    (When I was a child, I swam often.)
  • 日本にほんへ行いくときに、このかばんを買かいました。
    (I bought this bag [at the time] I was going to Japan.)
  • 暇ひまなときに、テレビを見みます。
    (When I am free, I watch TV.)

2. Tense of the Verb before 時

This is a subtle point for learners:

  • Dictionary Form + 時: Before the action is finished (e.g., "on the way to").
  • Ta-Form + 時: After the action is finished (e.g., "once arrived at").
