No Other Way: ～ほか（は）ない / ～しかない

Sometimes, life leaves you with only one path. When you have no choice but to do something, we use ～ほか（は）ない or ～しかない. It expresses a sense of resignation or firm determination because all other options are gone.

1. Sentence Structure

Verb (Dictionary Form) + ほか（は）ない / しかない

2. Resolute Examples

  • バスが止とまったので、歩あるいて帰かえるしかない。
    (The bus stopped, so there's nothing for it but to walk home.)
  • やるほかはない。頑張がんばろう！
    (We have no choice but to do it. Let's give it our all!)
  • 本当ほんとうのことを話はなすほか（は）なかった。
    (I had no choice but to tell the truth.)

3. Sensei's Note

～しかない is more common in daily conversation, while ～ほかはない sounds a bit more formal or written. Both carry that heavy feeling of "this is the only remaining option." Use it when you've reached the end of your rope!
