Speaking of...: ～といえば

The expression `～といえば` is used to pick up a topic that was just mentioned or to start talking about something associated with a word. It translates to "speaking of...", "if you say...", or "come to think of it."

1. Picking up a Topic

Use it when someone says something that reminds you of a related piece of information.

  • 「昨日きのう、京都きょうとへ行いきました。」
    「京都きょうと`といえば`、お寺てらがきれいですね。」
    ("I went to Kyoto yesterday." "`Speaking of` Kyoto, the temples are beautiful, aren't they?")

2. Identifying Representative Characteristics

Use it to mention the first thing that comes to mind when you hear a certain word.

  • 日本にほんの食たべもの`といえば`、お寿司すしが一番いちばんに思おもい浮うかびます。
    (`If you say` Japanese food, sushi comes to mind first.)

3. Connection

Attach `といえば` directly to `Nouns`.

`[Noun] + といえば`

4. Formal Variation

In very formal contexts, you might see `～といいましたら` or `～といいますと`.
