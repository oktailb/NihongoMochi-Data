The Particle か (Question Marker)

The particle か is placed at the very end of a sentence to turn it into a question. It functions much like a question mark (?) in English.

1. Basic Questions

In polite Japanese, you simply add か after です or ます. You do not need to change the word order of the sentence.

  • 学生がくせいですか。
    (Are you a student?)
  • 飲のみますか。
    (Will you drink?)
  • わかりましたか。
    (Did you understand?)

2. Using Question Words

When using words like "Who", "What", or "Where", the sentence still ends with か.

  • それは何なにですか。
    (What is that?)
  • 誰だれが来きましたか。
    (Who came?)

3. "A or B" (Choice)

You can use か between two nouns to mean "or".

  • コーヒーかお茶ちゃを飲のみます。
    (I will drink coffee or tea.)

4. Embedding Questions

In more advanced sentences, か can be used to mean "whether or not."

  • 行いくかわかりません。
    (I don't know if [I/someone] will go.)
