Numbers (数字すうじ)

Japanese numbers are logical, but they have a few "evolutionary branches" (read: exceptions) that you need to master, especially from 1 to 10.

2. Counters (助数詞じょすうし)

In Japanese, you don't just say "two books." You say "two (long-cylindrical-object-counter) books." It's like gear in an RPG: you need the right tool for the job!

The "Universal" Counter: ～つ (Tsu)

If you're in a panic at a restaurant, use these! They work for general objects from 1 to 10.

  • 1: 一つひとつ
  • 2: 二つふたつ
  • 3: 三つみっつ (Small 'tsu' appears!)
  • 10: 十とお (No 'tsu' at the end!)

Common Specialized Counters

  • ～本ほん: Long, cylindrical objects (pens, bottles, umbrellas, light sabers).
    Watch out! 1 is ippon, 3 is sanbon, 6 is roppon.
  • ～枚まい: Flat objects (paper, shirts, plates, pizza). Easy! No weird changes.
  • ～個こ: Small round objects (apples, dice, orbs). 1 is ikko, 6 is rokko.

4. People and Age

Counting people is like a boss battle. 1 and 2 are unique.

  • 1 Person: 一人ひとり (Hitori - Solo)
  • 2 People: 二人ふたり (Futari - Duo)
  • 3+ People: [Number] + 人にん (Example: 三人さんにん)

Age (歳 / 才さい): 1 year old is issai, 8 is hassai, 10 is jussai.
And the ultimate exception: 20 years old is 二十歳はたち!

5. The "Big League" (100+)

Watch out for the special readings in 100 and 1000!

  • 100: 百ひゃく (Hyaku)
    • 300: 三百さんびゃく (San-byaku)
    • 600: 六百ろっぴゃく (Roppyaku)
    • 800: 八百ハッピャク (Happyaku)
  • 1,000: 千せん (Sen)
    • 3,000: 三千さんぜん (San-zen)
    • 8,000: 八千はっせん (Hassen)
  • 10,000: 万まん (Man) - Japanese counts in groups of 4 zeros, not 3!

Sensei's Tip: Don't try to memorize all 500+ counters at once. Start with things you actually use, like bottles of sake or people in your party! 🍶🛡️✨
