To Begin: ～はじめる

The suffix `～はじめる` (written as `～始める`) is used to express that an action or a process has started. Unlike `～だす`, it is used for intentional or natural starts.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `はじめる` to the `Verb Stem` (the part before `~ます`).

`[Verb Stem] + はじめる`

  • 日本語にほんごを`勉強べんきょうしはじめました`。
    (I started studying Japanese.)
  • 雨あめが`降ふりはじめました`。
    (It started to rain.)
  • 本ほんを`読よみはじめましょう`。
    (Let's start reading the book.)

2. Conjugation

Once `はじめる` is attached, it behaves like a Group 2 (Ichidan) verb.

  • `Polite:` ～しはじめます
  • `Past:` ～しはじめました
  • `Negative:` ～しはじめない
