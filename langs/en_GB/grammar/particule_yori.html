The Particle より (Than / From)

The particle `より` is primarily used for comparisons, but it can also indicate a starting point in formal or written contexts.

1. Comparison (Than)

When comparing two things, `より` follows the thing that is "less" of the quality being described. It translates to "more than..."

  • 飛行機ひこうきは新幹線しんかんせん`より`速はやいです。
    (Airplanes are faster `than` bullet trains.)
  • コーヒー`より`お茶ちゃのほうが好すきです。
    (I like tea more `than` coffee.)

2. Formal Starting Point (From)

In formal letters or announcements, `より` can replace `から` to mean "from."

  • 佐藤さとう`より`
    (From Sato [often used at the end of a letter or on a gift tag].)

3. The structure: A より B のほうが

This is the standard way to compare two things in Japanese.

  • `[Less] より [More] のほうが [Adjective] です。`
    夏なつ`より`冬ふゆのほうが好すきです。
    (I like winter more than summer.)
