Ever since: ～て以来

The expression `～て以来` (ていらい) is used to say that a certain state has continued without change from a point in the past until the present.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `以来` to the `Te-form` of a verb or to a `Noun` representing a point in time.

  • 日本にほんに来く`て以来`、ずっと忙いそがしいです。
    (Ever since I came to Japan, I have been busy.)
  • かれは三年前さんねんまえの事故じこ`以来`、お酒さけを飲のんでいません。
    (Ever since the accident three years ago, he has not drunk alcohol.)
  • 卒業そつぎょう`して以来`、かれには一度いちども会あっていません。
    (Ever since graduating, I haven't met him even once.)

2. Key Nuance: Continuity

Unlike `～てから` (which just means "after"), `～て以来` emphasizes that the state has remained `constant` or `unchanged` throughout the entire period from the starting point until now.

3. Important Restriction

You generally don't use `～て以来` for very recent events. It is best used for events that happened some time ago and have established a long-term pattern.
