Giving Reasons: ～から and ～ので

In Japanese, to say "because" or "so," we place the reason `before` the result. There are two main ways to express this: `から` and `ので`.

1. ～から (Subjective Reason)

`から` is used when expressing a personal reason, opinion, or when making a request or command. It is more subjective and forceful than ので.

`[Reason] から、[Result]。`

  • 暑あつい`から`、窓まどを開あけます。
    (Because it is hot, I will open the window.)
  • 時間じかんがない`から`、急いそぎましょう。
    (Since there is no time, let's hurry.)

2. ～ので (Objective/Polite Reason)

`ので` is used for objective facts, social excuses, or to be extra polite. It sounds less forceful and more like a natural consequence.

`[Reason] ので、[Result]。`

  • 雨あめが降ふっている`ので`、行いきません。
    (Because it is raining [objective fact], I won't go.)
  • 用事ようじがある`ので`、お先さきに失礼しつれいします。
    (Since I have an errand, I will excuse myself first.)

3. How to Connect

Type With から With ので
Verbs / I-Adj Dictionary Form + から Dictionary Form + ので
Nouns / Na-Adj だ + から `な` + ので

4. Quick Tip

If you are apologizing or asking for a favor, use `ので`. It sounds much softer and more professional!
