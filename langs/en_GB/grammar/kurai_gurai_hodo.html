Approximation and Degree: くらい, ぐらい, and ほど

These three words are used to express "about," "approximately," or the "extent" of something. While they are often interchangeable, they have subtle differences in formality and nuance.

1. くらい / ぐらい (About / Approximately)

Both are used after a quantity or duration. ぐらい is slightly more common in spoken Japanese.

  • 三時間さんじかんぐらい寝ねました。
    (I slept for about three hours.)
  • この鞄かばんは一万円いちまんえんくらいです。
    (This bag is about 10,000 yen.)

2. ほど (To the extent of / About)

ほど is more formal than kurai. It is often used to emphasize a large degree or to make comparisons.

  • 昨日きのうは死しぬほど忙いそがしかったです。
    (Yesterday I was busy to the point of dying / extremely busy.)
  • 今年ことしは去年きょねんほど暑あつくないです。
    (This year is not as hot as last year.)

3. Summary of Comparison

Word Primary Use Formality
くらい / ぐらい General approximations of time/amount. Casual / Standard
ほど High degree, formal approximations, comparisons. Formal / Emphatic

4. Quick Tip: ぐらい vs ごろ

Remember: Use ぐらい for durations (3 hours) and ごろ for specific points in time (3 o'clock).
