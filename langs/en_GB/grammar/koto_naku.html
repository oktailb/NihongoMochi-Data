Doing Without Doing: ～ことなく

In life, sometimes the smoothest path is one where an expected action doesn't happen. ～ことなく is a formal way to say "without doing X." It's the sophisticated cousin of ~naide.

1. Sentence Structure

Verb (Dictionary Form) + ことなく

2. Smooth Examples

  • 彼かれは一度いちども休やすむことなく、４２キロを走はしりぬいた。
    (He ran the 42km without resting even once.)
  • 時計とけいは止とまることなく、時ときを刻きざみ続つづけている。
    (The clock continues to tick away without stopping.)
  • 誰だれにも相談そうだんすることなく、一人ひとりで決きめてしまった。
    (I decided by myself without consulting anyone.)

3. Sensei's Note

While ~naide is used in everyday speech, ～ことなく appears frequently in literature, news, and formal speeches. It sounds more continuous and stable. Use it when you want to describe a process that flowed perfectly without a specific interruption!
