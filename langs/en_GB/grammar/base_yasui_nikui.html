Easy and Difficult: ～やすい and ～にくい

In Japanese, you can express how easy or difficult an action is by adding these suffixes to the verb stem.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach やすい (easy) or にくい (difficult) to the Verb Stem.

  • このペンは書かきやすいです。
    (This pen is easy to write with.)
  • その漢字かんじは覚おぼえにくいです。
    (That Kanji is difficult to remember.)
  • ここは住すみやすい町まちですね。
    (This is an easy town to live in, isn't it?)

2. Conjugation

Once attached, the entire word behaves like an I-adjective.

  • Negative: ～やすくない / ～にくくない
  • Past: ～やすかった / ～にくかった

3. Nuance

These suffixes describe the inherent quality of the object. For example, "this book is easy to read" because of its large font or simple language.
