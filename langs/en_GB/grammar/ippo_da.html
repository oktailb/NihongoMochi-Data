Continuous Change: ～いっぽうだ

The expression ～いっぽうだ (written as ～一方だ) is used to describe a situation that continues to change in one direction. It is most commonly used for negative trends that are getting worse.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach いっぽうだ to the Dictionary Form of a verb that expresses change (like increase, decrease, or worsen).

[Verb Dictionary Form] + いっぽうだ

  • 物価ぶっかは上あがるいっぽうだ。
    (Prices just keep on rising.)
  • 不景気ふけいきのせいで、仕事しごとは減へるいっぽうだ。
    (Due to the recession, work just keeps on decreasing.)
  • 祖父そふの病気びょうきは悪わるくなるいっぽうです。
    (My grandfather's illness is only getting worse.)

2. Key Verbs used with ～いっぽうだ

  • 増ふえる (to increase) / 減へる (to decrease)
  • 上あがる (to rise) / 下さがる (to fall)
  • 悪わるくなる (to worsen) / 忙いそがしくなる (to get busier)

3. Nuance

It implies that once the change has started, it doesn't stop and continues steadily in that direction. It is often translated as "keeps on...", "continues to...", or "nothing but...".
