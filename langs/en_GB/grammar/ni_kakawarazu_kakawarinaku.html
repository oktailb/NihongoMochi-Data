Regardless of Factors: ～にかかわらず / ～に（は）かかわりなく

Sometimes a rule or a truth applies to everyone and everything, no matter the circumstances. ～にかかわらず (written as ～に関わらず) means "regardless of" or "it doesn't matter if..." It's used when a situation is constant across different variables.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Noun + にかかわらず
  • Verb (Dictionary/Negative Form) + にかかわらず

2. Constant Examples

  • この大会たいかいは年齢ねんれいにかかわらず、だれでも参加さんかできます。
    (This tournament is open to anyone, regardless of age.)
  • 好すきか嫌きらいかにかかわらず、やらなければならない。
    (Regardless of whether you like it or not, you have to do it.)
  • 天候てんこうにかかわらず、試合しあいは行おこなわれます。
    (The match will be held regardless of the weather.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a very fair and objective expression! It's often used in official rules, announcements, or when talking about universal requirements. It says: "The factor X has zero influence on the outcome Y."
