Sudden Action: ～だす

The suffix `～だす` (written as `～出す`) is attached to a verb to express that an action has started suddenly or unexpectedly.

1. Sentence Structure

Attach `だす` to the `Verb Stem` (the part before `~ます`).

`[Verb Stem] + だす`

  • 急きゅうに雨あめが`降ふりだしました`。
    (Suddenly, it started raining.)
  • 赤あかちゃんが`泣なきだした`。
    (The baby suddenly started crying.)
  • みんなが`笑わらいだした`。
    (Everyone burst out laughing.)

2. Nuance: Suddenness

Compared to `～はじめる` (to begin), `～だす` emphasizes that the start was sudden, impulsive, or unexpected.
