Heightened Results: ～だけに

Similar to ~dake atte, ～だけに is used when something is true precisely because of a certain reason. However, ～だけに often emphasizes that the result is even more intense (better or worse) because of that reason.

1. Sentence Structure

  • Noun + だけに
  • Verb/Adjective (Plain form) + だけに
  • な-Adj + な + だけに

2. Deep Examples

  • 期待きたいしていただけに、失敗しっぱいしたときはとても悲かなしかった。
    (Precisely because I had high expectations, I was even more sad when I failed.)
  • 彼かれは若わかいだけに、吸収きゅうしゅうが早はやい。
    (Precisely because he is young, his ability to learn is even faster.)
  • この仕事しごとは大変たいへんなだけに、やりがいがある。
    (Precisely because this job is so difficult, it is all the more rewarding.)

3. Sensei's Note

While ~dake atte is mostly for praise, ～だけに is more versatile. It can be used for negative situations to show why the pain or disappointment is "extra." It connects the cause to a natural, but amplified, effect.
