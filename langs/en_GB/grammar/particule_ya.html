The Particle や (Non-exhaustive "And")

The particle `や` is used to list nouns when you want to imply that there are other things in the group besides the ones you mentioned. It translates to "A, B, and so on."

1. Sentence Structure

Place `や` between nouns. It is often used with `など` at the end of the list.

  • 机つくえの上うえに本ほん`や`ペンがあります。
    (There are books, pens, [among other things] on the desk.)
  • スーパーで肉にく`や`野菜やさいを買かいました。
    (I bought meat, vegetables, and so on at the supermarket.)

2. Difference from と

  • `と:` Used for a complete list (A and B, that's all).
  • `や:` Used for an incomplete list (A, B, and others).

3. Usage with など

It is very common to see `や` and `など` paired together to clearly show the list is just a few examples.

  • かばんの中に財布さいふ`や`鍵かぎ`など`が入っています。
    (There are things like a wallet and keys in the bag.)
