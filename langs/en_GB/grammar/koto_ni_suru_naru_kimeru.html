Decisions: ～ことにする and ～ことになる

These expressions describe decisions. One focuses on your own will, while the other focuses on external decisions or situations.

1. Personal Decision: ～ことにする

Use this when you have made a conscious choice to do (or not do) something.

  • 健康けんこうのために、毎日まいにち走はしる`ことにしました`。
    (I have decided to run every day for my health.)
  • あまりおもしろくないので、見みない`ことにしました`。
    (I've decided not to watch it because it's not very interesting.)

2. External Decision / Result: ～ことになる

Use this when a decision has been made by someone else, an organization, or as a natural result of circumstances.

  • 来月らいげつ、日本にほんへ出張しゅっちょうする`ことになりました`。
    (It has been decided that I will go on a business trip to Japan next month.)
  • この学校がっこうでは、日本語にほんごしか話はなしてはいけない`ことになっています`。
    (In this school, it is a rule that you must only speak Japanese.)
