Satisfaction and Relief: ～てよかった

There is no better feeling than a positive result! `～てよかった` (written as `～てよかった`) is used to express satisfaction, happiness, or relief that a certain event happened or a certain action was taken. It means "I'm glad that..." or "It was good that..."

1. Sentence Structure

`[Verb Te-form] + よかった`

2. Grateful Examples

  • 日本にほんへ来`てよかった`。
    (I'm glad I came to Japan.)
  • 彼かれに会え`てよかった`。
    (I'm glad I was able to meet him.)
  • 傘かさを持ってき`てよかった`。雨が降ってきた。
    (I'm glad I brought an umbrella. It started raining.)

3. Sensei's Note

This is a wonderful way to express gratitude for a situation. It's often used when reflecting on a choice you made in the past that turned out to be the right one. For even more relief, you can say `～て安心した` (`~て　あんしん　した` - I'm relieved that...).
